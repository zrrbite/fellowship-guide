# Urrak Markets — Adventure (dual boss)

**Bosses:** Slavetrader Brull & Drazhul · **Length:** 10-15 min · **Difficulty:** **C — the hardest Adventure**

**Drops:** Tuzari Grace (+4% Haste, +20% movement speed)

> **Recommendation for our group: leave this one alone for now.** The trash is moderately difficult
> and the final encounter is extremely demanding — it needs strong positioning, coordinated
> interrupts and disciplined defensive use, all at once. Overgear rates its farming value as
> "limited". Come back when you are overgeared and want the challenge.

## Route

At the **three-way fork** early on, **take the right path**. Avoid the centre path toward the boss
until you are ready. Left and right connect in a circle with the same mob types.

## Trash that kills casual groups

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Gorestained Butcher** | **CC-immune.** **Furious Tantrum** channels heavy group damage. | Coordinated healing — there is no interrupt answer |
| **Desecrator** | **Infernal Explosive** circle must be soaked | **Someone claims it.** Failure is massive group damage. |
| **Hellcaller** | **Restore Vitality** heals mobs | Interrupt it |
| **Urrak Slaver** | Barbed Chain | Interrupt or CC |
| **Urrak Witch** | Mossfire Bolt, targets the tank | Interrupt |
| **Horned Seer** | Pain Bolt, targets the tank | Interrupt |

That is four separate interrupt targets in one dungeon. This is why it is rated hardest — it demands
more simultaneous interrupt discipline than any other Adventure.

## Bosses: Drazhul and Slavetrader Brull

**The core rule: cleave both bosses evenly.** The higher-health boss gains a **stacking 2% damage
increase for every 1% more health it has** than the other. Letting them drift apart makes the fight
progressively unwinnable.

| Mechanic | Source | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Vortex** | Drazhul | Pulls players to the arena centre for lethal damage. **Stand in Wildroot Thorns puddles to resist the pull.** |
| **Wildroot Thorns** | Brull | Damaging root puddles | **Position them between the centre and the walls** so they are available as Vortex anchors |
| **Conjure Scythe** | — | Spinning blades applying bleed on contact | Direct them away from the group |
| **Infernal Explosive** | — | Circles needing a soak each | One player per circle |
| **Wild Bolt / Creeping Death** | Brull | Heavy tank pressure | Interrupt Wild Bolt; save mitigation for the uninterruptible Creeping Death |

### The three things to get right
1. **Keep the health bars level.** Everything else is downstream of this.
2. **Use the thorns as Vortex cover.** The puddles that look like a hazard are actually the solution.
3. **Claim your explosive circle out loud.** With this much going on, assumed soaks do not happen.
