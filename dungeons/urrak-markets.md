# Urrak Markets — Adventure (dual boss)

**Bosses:** Slavetrader Brull & Drazhul the Fleshbroker · **Length:** 10-15 min · **Difficulty:** **C — the hardest Adventure**

**Drops:** Tuzari Grace (+4% Haste, +20% movement speed)

> **Recommendation for our group: leave this one alone for now.** The trash is moderately difficult
> and the final encounter is extremely demanding — it needs strong positioning, coordinated
> interrupts and disciplined defensive use, all at once. Overgear rates its farming value as
> "limited". Come back when you are overgeared and want the challenge.

## Route

At the **three-way fork** early on, **take the right path**. Avoid the centre path toward the boss
until you are ready. Left and right connect in a circle with the same mob types.

## Trash that kills casual groups

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Gorestained Butcher** | **CC-immune.** **Furious Tantrum** channels heavy group damage. | Coordinated healing — there is no interrupt answer |
| **Desecrator** | **Infernal Explosive** circle must be soaked | **Someone claims it.** Failure is massive group damage. |
| **Hellcaller** | **Restore Vitality** heals mobs | Interrupt it — highest trash priority |
| **Urrak Slaver** | **Barred Chain** *(Adept+)* | Interrupt or CC |
| **Urrak Witch** | **Mossfire Bolt**, **Grasping Vines**, **Jungle Rot** | Interrupt; Mossfire Bolt targets the tank |
| **Horned Seer** | **Pain Bolt**, **Orb of Blood**; **Sanguine Offering** *(Adept+)* | Interrupt |

That is four separate interrupt targets in one dungeon, and Adept+ adds two more casts on top. This
is why it is rated hardest — it demands more simultaneous interrupt discipline than any other
Adventure.

## Bosses: Slavetrader Brull and Drazhul the Fleshbroker

**The core rule: cleave both bosses evenly.** Each gains **Enrage — 2% more damage for every 1% more
health it has** than the other. Letting them drift apart makes the fight progressively unwinnable.

### Slavetrader Brull

| Ability | Recast | Interruptible | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Wild Bolt** | short | **Yes** | Short cast, heavy damage on the main target | **Kick it freely.** It is the *only* interruptible cast in the entire encounter, so there is nothing to bank for |
| **Creeping Decay** | 20s | No | Uninterruptible heavy damage on the current target — the tank buster | Save mitigation for it. **Do not press an interrupt at this** |
| **Wildroot Thorns** | **60s** | No | Spawns a patch of vines at a **random party member other than the tank** | **Position them between the centre and the walls** — see below |
| **Enrage** | passive | No | Scales with the health gap between bosses | Keep the bars level |

### Drazhul the Fleshbroker

| Ability | Recast | Interruptible | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Conjure Scythe** | **15s** | No | 2.5s cast, then a scythe thrown at the **furthest** party member; it ricochets off terrain and applies a 12s DoT on contact | **The furthest player owns this.** Whoever is playing at max range should expect every scythe and steer it away from the group |
| **Furious Tantrum** | 30s | No | Channels heavy party-wide damage every 2s for 4 seconds | Group cooldowns. There is no interrupt answer |
| **Vortex** | **60s** | No | Leaps to the arena centre, 3s cast, then channels, sucking in all players **and scythes** | **Stand in Wildroot Thorns puddles to resist the pull** |
| **Infernal Explosive** *(Vayr's Legacy — Adept+)* | **60s** | No | Bomb tossed near a random player; heavy party damage if not soaked | **Claim your circle out loud** |
| **Enrage** | passive | No | Scales with the health gap between bosses | Keep the bars level |

### The 60-second convergence

Wildroot Thorns, Vortex and Infernal Explosive **all sit on a 60-second cycle**. That is not
coincidence — the fight is built so that Brull's thorns are the answer to Drazhul's Vortex. Two
consequences:

1. **Place thorns one Vortex ahead.** When Wildroot Thorns lands, that patch is the anchor you will
   stand in at the next Vortex. Put it where you want to be, not where you happen to be.
2. **At Adept and above, Infernal Explosive lands in the same window.** You are soaking a bomb while
   being pulled to the centre. Decide *before* the pull who breaks off to soak and who anchors.

Meanwhile Conjure Scythe ticks every 15 seconds throughout, so there are always scythes in the room
when Vortex fires — that is what it is sucking in.

### The three things to get right
1. **Keep the health bars level.** Everything else is downstream of this.
2. **Use the thorns as Vortex cover, and place them deliberately.** The puddles that look like a
   hazard are the solution, but only if they are in the right place.
3. **Claim your explosive circle out loud.** With this much going on, assumed soaks do not happen.

> **Correction:** earlier notes here called Brull's tank buster *Creeping Death*. The ability is
> **Creeping Decay**, and it is **not** interruptible.
