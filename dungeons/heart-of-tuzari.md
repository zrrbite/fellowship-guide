# The Heart of Tuzari — Capstone (3 bosses)

**Bosses:** Moar'Gore Master of Sacrifice, Vun'Kahr the Thorned Maw, Prophet Ez'rath
**Kill Score:** 201 · **Timer:** 28 minutes

**Drops:** Dark Prophecy (+25% Haste procs), plus **unique set pieces and relics found nowhere else**

An ancient jungle temple suffused with demonic energy. Worth running despite the difficulty, because
of the exclusive loot.

## Route

**Take the right path around the flame gate**, past the Lasher mobs, to reach the second boss. It is
only a couple of pulls compared with the left path — a meaningful saving against a 28-minute timer.

## Trash that kills casual groups

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Hellcaller** | **Restore Vitality** heals mobs; also Demonic Lash | **Interrupt or CC Restore Vitality.** With a 28-minute timer you cannot afford mobs healing. |
| **Barbarous Lasher** | **CC-immune.** **Wicked Thorns** reflects **25% of all damage taken** back at the attacker. | Expect the reflect; do not panic-burst it while low |
| **Executioner** | **CC-immune.** Damage scales with your missing health; melee cleaves. | **Tank faces away from the group**; keep people topped |
| **Hulking Bulgore** | **CC-immune.** Charges when kited; **enrages at 50%** with more damage and haste. | Do not kite it. Commit and kill it. |
| **Barbed Roots** | **Toxic Spores** — channels group damage **if no melee is in range** | Keep a melee on them; interrupt the channel |
| **Ashen Arsonist** | **Flame Blast** (frontal cone), **Big Oogha Flame** (DoT on a random player) | Interrupt both |
| **Profane Invoker** | **Shadow Bolt** at the tank, **Wither** on a random player | Interrupt both |

## Boss 1: Moar'Gore, Master of Sacrifice

Heavy pressure on tank and healer, and it demands real teamwork.

> **Do not interrupt Sacrificial Pyre.** It is the one interruptible cast on this boss, which makes
> it a magnet for a reflex kick — but **the flames are how you clear Tainted Blood stacks**. Kicking
> it removes your own stack-clearing tool. Tell the group explicitly, because "interruptible" and
> "should be interrupted" are not the same thing here.

- **Siphon Blood** channels into pillars, spawning **bouncing orbs**. Below 30% he channels into
  **all 5 pillars instead of 3**.
- **If an orb hits Moar'Gore it deals damage to the entire party.** If it hits a player, that player
  takes damage and **+20% damage taken per stack**.
- **The tank moves the boss away from orbs; everyone else soaks them.**
- **Sacrificial Pyre** *(interruptible — leave it alone)* — sets a large area alight for huge damage
  on eruption and a DoT to anyone who enters afterwards. **Step in deliberately to clear stacks.**
- **Hemorrhaging Strike** — wounding blow on the tank applying a DoT. Tank defensive.
- **Gets significantly more dangerous below 30%** — save cooldowns for it.

## Boss 2: Vun'Kahr, the Thorned Maw

Movement and add control. **Nothing here is interruptible.**

- **Legion of Vun'Kahr** spawns seedlings. **Soak them** or they become **Dormant Slaughtervines**.
  **Assign players to arena sections** so nothing is missed.
- **Raise Terrorvines** spawns **Barbed Roots**, whose life force is linked to the boss: **40% of the
  damage you deal to a root is also dealt to Vun'Kahr.** Cleaving roots is not a distraction from the
  boss, it *is* damage on the boss — and **Barbed Roots channel Toxic Spores for group damage if no
  melee is in range**, so someone should be on them regardless.
- **Fangroot Snap** is a tank buster that **also cleaves anyone in front of the boss**.
- **Thornmother's Slumber** activates any dormant adds *and* increases boss damage taken — so the
  cleaner your soaking, the better this window is.

## Boss 3: Prophet Ez'rath

**Nothing here is interruptible.**

- A central **Demonic Pit** puddle slows and amplifies damage taken. **Do not let the boss enter it.**
- **Ez'rath's Chosen** — four adds march toward the Pit. Each one that arrives applies a stacking
  **30-second Sacrificial Offering DoT** to the whole party. **Kill at least 2-3 of them.**
- **Dark Malice** conjures a circle around him dealing huge damage to anyone caught when it ends.
- **Wicked Hook** targets a random player for damage and a **65% slow** — and it **stops entirely
  below 50%**, so the second half is a different fight from the first.
- **At 50%, Abyssal Nexus** erupts **four rotating beams** from the Pit, which makes add-killing much
  harder. Save burst for this, and note you are trading Wicked Hook away to get it.

### The three things to get right
1. **Soak everything.** All three bosses are soak-checks in different costumes. A group that soaks
   reliably clears this; a group that does not, does not.
2. **Interrupt Restore Vitality in the trash.** The Kill Score of 201 and a 28-minute timer leave no
   room for mobs healing up.
3. **Save burst for the sub-50% phases** on Moar'Gore and Ez'rath. Both get materially harder late.
