# Scryer's Peak — Adventure (1 boss)

**Boss:** Overlord Varux · **Length:** 10-15 min · **Difficulty:** Easy · **Added:** Season 3

> ## Farm this one
> Scryer's Peak is one of only two Adventures that drop **Seal of the Heskyr** (+25% Gem Power),
> which is best-in-slot for every hero in the game. It is also easy. For a once-a-week group this is
> the highest value per minute available anywhere.

## Route

No single forced path. Load a route from the [Method Route Planner](https://www.method.gg/fellowship/route-planner)
once and run the same one every week — consistency is worth more to us than optimality.

## Trash that kills casual groups

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Heskyr Magus** | **Sanguine Maelstrom** — channelled large AoE every second. Also applies a dispellable stacking debuff. | Interrupt Sanguine Maelstrom and Blood Bolt. Dispel before it reaches 10 stacks. |
| **Sanguine Disciple** | Channels **Sanguine Ritual** at a player for ticking damage | Interrupt it — this also weakens the adds it spawns |
| **Stalking Grim** | **Piercing Howl** silences casters. **Blood Scent** is a stacking aura giving nearby mobs +10% damage, haste and max HP. | Kill or control early. The aura compounds badly in long pulls. |

**Interrupt priority:** Sanguine Ritual > Sanguine Maelstrom > Blood Bolt.

## Boss: Overlord Varux

| Mechanic | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Spirit Strike** | Tank debuff, stacks damage on repeat casts | Tank rotates defensives across casts |
| **Blood Link** | Two players tethered | Break it with distance. It stuns — expect it, do not panic. |
| **Stampede** | Wolf pack charges through | Dodge. The debuff hurts badly if you eat it. |
| **Bloodmarked / Crimson Assault** | Frontal cones | Spread so cones do not overlap the group |
| **Rain of Blood** | Bloods spawn and travel toward Varux | **Soak them.** You get buffs; if they reach Varux, he does. |
| **Fatalistic Ritual** | Intermission | Break the shield and interrupt the spell |

### The three things to get right
1. **Soak every blood.** This is the fight. Letting them reach Varux is how the fight gets hard.
2. **Break Blood Link fast** — assign the pair to run opposite directions on sight.
3. **Spread for cones.** Stacking for convenience is what turns a clean pull into a wipe.
