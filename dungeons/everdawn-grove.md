# Everdawn Grove — Adventure

**Boss:** Malgut the Fetid · **Length:** 10-15 min · **Difficulty:** B to C — **hard for casual groups**

**Drops:** Tuzari Grace (+4% Haste, +20% movement speed)

> **Recommendation for our group: skip this until overgeared.** Everdawn Grove contains many
> dangerous casters, and **Malgut heavily punishes positioning mistakes with full wipes**. The only
> set here is Tuzari Grace, which is a nice-to-have rather than a need. Method rates it B; Overgear
> rates it C. Either way it is not where a Tuesday night is best spent.

## Route

**Take the left path** after the opening trash. Both paths connect, with the same mobs in different
combinations. There are **skippable elites** here — skipping them is correct.

## Trash that kills casual groups

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Desecrator** | **Volley** creates random target circles. In Vayr's Legacy, an **Infernal Explosive** circle must be soaked. | Interrupt or CC the Volley channel. **Someone must soak the explosive** or the group takes heavy damage. |
| **Skittershard** | **On death** applies a stacking DoT with healing reduction to everyone | Healer dispels fast; a **Mass Dispel relic** is close to mandatory if you pull several |
| **Corrupted Totemic** | Spawns Bloodstone Totems that spam damage | CC the Corrupted Prayer channel, interrupt Shadow Lob |
| **Bloodstone Goliath** | **CC-immune.** Earthcore Upheaval circles; gains Coalescing Bloodstone stacks. | Dodge circles, defensives as stacks build |

## Boss: Malgut the Fetid

This fight involves an **NPC ally, Akari the Everdawn Lord**, who both patrols and can be aimed. Most
of the difficulty is learning to use him.

| Mechanic | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Corrupting Shot** | Magic debuff on random players | **Group up before dispelling** to split the burst |
| **Crystal Volley** | Cone at all four players | Spread loosely — and **keep Akari out of the cones** |
| **Bloodstone Totems** | Two spawn under Vayr's Legacy | Focus one; **aim Akari's charge at the other** to delete it instantly |
| **Umbral Eruption** | Heavy pulsing damage with a safe zone behind the boss | **Tank moves the safe spot along Akari's patrol path**; everyone stays in it |
| **Godrot Eclipse** | Party-wide damage until interrupted | **Aim Akari's charge stun at Malgut** to stop it |

### The three things to get right
1. **Akari is a tool, not scenery.** His charge deletes totems and interrupts Godrot Eclipse.
   Groups that ignore him find this fight nearly impossible.
2. **Group up to dispel Corrupting Shot.** Splitting the burst is the difference between a scare and
   a death.
3. **Keep Akari out of Crystal Volley.** Losing him loses the fight.
