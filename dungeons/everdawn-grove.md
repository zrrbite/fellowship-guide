# Everdawn Grove — Adventure

**Boss:** Malgut the Fetid · **Length:** 10-15 min · **Difficulty:** B to C — **hard for casual groups**

**Drops:** Tuzari Grace (+4% Haste, +20% movement speed)

> **Recommendation for our group: skip this until overgeared.** Everdawn Grove contains many
> dangerous casters, and **Malgut heavily punishes positioning mistakes with full wipes**. The only
> set here is Tuzari Grace, which is a nice-to-have rather than a need. Method rates it B; Overgear
> rates it C. Either way it is not where a Tuesday night is best spent.

## Route

**Take the left path** after the opening trash. Both paths connect, with the same mobs in different
combinations. There are **skippable elites** here — skipping them is correct.

## Trash that kills casual groups

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Desecrator** | **Volley** creates random target circles. Under Vayr's Legacy, an **Infernal Explosive** circle must be soaked. | Interrupt or CC the Volley channel. **Someone must soak the explosive** or the group takes heavy damage. |
| **Skittershard** | **On death** applies a stacking DoT with healing reduction to everyone | Healer dispels fast; a **Mass Dispel relic** is close to mandatory if you pull several |
| **Corrupted Totemic** | **Shadow Lob** (interrupt — priority) and **Restoration** (interrupt — it heals) | Interrupt both. Also CC the Corrupted Prayer channel. |
| **Horned Seer** | **Pain Bolt** and **Orb of Blood**; **Sanguine Offering** is a priority interrupt added at Adept+ | Interrupt all three. Sanguine Offering first once you are past Contender. |
| **Bloodstone Goliath** | **CC-immune.** Earthcore Upheaval circles; gains Coalescing Bloodstone stacks. | Dodge circles, defensives as stacks build |

## Boss: Malgut the Fetid

This fight involves an **NPC ally, Akari the Everdawn Lord**, who both patrols and can be aimed. Most
of the difficulty is learning to use him.

> **Nothing Malgut casts is interruptible.** Godrot Eclipse is stopped by **Akari's charge stun**,
> not by a kick — pressing your interrupt at it does nothing. This is the single most common
> misunderstanding of the fight.

| Ability | Recast | Interruptible | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Summon Totems** *(Vayr's Legacy — Adept+)* | **18.5s** | No | Two totems spawn and continuously damage the whole party | Focus one; **aim Akari's charge at the other** to delete it instantly |
| **Crystal Volley** | 23s | No | 3s channel, then a cone centred on **each** party member | Spread loosely — and **keep Akari out of the cones** |
| **Umbral Eruption** | 28s | No | Crescent-shaped attack pulsing heavy damage over 4s, with a safe zone behind the boss | **Tank moves the safe spot along Akari's patrol path**; everyone stays in it |
| **Godrot Eclipse** | 30s | **No** | Lethal party-wide damage after **7.5 seconds** | **Aim Akari's charge stun at Malgut.** You have 7.5s — it is a long window, but only if someone owns the job |
| **Corrupting Shot** | **60s** | No | DoT debuff that centres an AoE soak on the target | **Group up before dispelling** to split the burst |
| **Kill Shot** | — | No | Fires on Akari once the whole party is dead | Nothing. This is the wipe animation. |

### The three things to get right
1. **Akari is a tool, not scenery.** His charge deletes totems and stuns Malgut out of Godrot
   Eclipse. Groups that ignore him find this fight nearly impossible.
2. **Group up to dispel Corrupting Shot.** Splitting the burst is the difference between a scare and
   a death.
3. **Keep Akari out of Crystal Volley.** Losing him loses the fight.

> **Shared timing skeleton:** Malgut and Sin-Magir in [Empyrean Sands](empyrean-sands.md) use the
> **identical** recast set — 18.5 / 23 / 28 / 30 / 60 seconds — with the 18.5s slot being the
> Vayr's Legacy addition in both. Learning the rhythm of one teaches the other.
