# Wraithtide Vault — Capstone (3 bosses)

**Bosses:** First Mate Marrow, Bael'Aurum, Deathless Katrine · **Length:** 28-32 min

**League role:** the **third capstone** — **timing it promotes you to Paragon**
**Drops:** Torment of Bael'Aurum (+4% primary stat, cheat-death heal), plus **exclusive relics**

Pirates and otherworldly enemies. This is the run that gets you into Paragon, which is where
Legendaries realistically start dropping, so it is worth real effort.

## Route

**Head right toward First Mate Marrow first**, then through the jungle to **Bael'Aurum**, and
finally **Deathless Katrine**. The dungeon is a **large loop**, so you meet all the trash regardless
of which way you start — the order is about boss difficulty pacing, not skipping content.

## Trash that kills casual groups

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Greedbound Prism** | **Mindrender Barrage** — heavy damage to the entire group | **Interrupt or CC this channel.** Highest priority in the dungeon. |
| **Greedspawn Sentinel** | **Greed's Embrace** reduces mob damage taken by **80%** | Interrupt it or the pull takes five times as long |
| **Scuttlejack Cannoneer** | **Shackle Blast** (chains) and **Blunder Boom** (bomb circles) | Interrupt or CC |
| **Bilgerat** | **Vermin Tide** spawns Gunpowder Rats that deal heavy area damage | Interrupt the channel immediately |
| **Tide Witch** | **Sunken Gale** and **Dreadwater Bolt** | Interrupt — she also spawns from Marrow's Cannonball Troops |
| **Soulpowder Buccaneer** | **Soulpowder Shot** | Interrupt |

## Boss 1: First Mate Marrow

**Nothing Marrow casts is interruptible** — save your kicks for the Tide Witch he summons.

- **Wavestomp** — large frontal cone for high damage, cutting movement speed by **70%** and haste by
  **30%**. **Tank does not stack with DPS.**
- **Anchor Strike** — cleaves everyone **within 4 yards of the tank**. Melee stay spread off the
  tank's hitbox; this is quiet, constant damage that groups never diagnose.
- **Cannonball Troops** — summons 2 Saltsworn Scallywags and 1 Tide Witch, **and grants Marrow a
  Reinforced buff**. Run an **interrupt rotation on the Tide Witch**.
- **Keelhaul** — two soak circles. **Stack for them, but do not over-soak**: missed soaks apply a
  stacking magic DoT **and healing reduction**, and extra bodies gain nothing.
- **High Tide** intermission — tosses an anchor to the arena centre and the tide rises. **Click the
  anchor to tether yourself** or you drown, then avoid the sharks swimming in lines.

## Boss 2: Bael'Aurum
An unusual fight: **no tanking required**.

> **Two of his casts are interruptible, and this page previously said neither was.**
> **Shadowgreed Eclipse** and **Hoard** can both be kicked. This changes the fight completely.

- **Hoard** *(interruptible)* — consumes the Cursed Treasure piles and the Orb, **healing Bael'Aurum
  in proportion to how much treasure he eats**. The tank's job is to **push the Orb of Bael'Aurum
  over Cursed Treasure piles** to clear them first, so there is less to consume — and **if the piles
  are still up when he starts, kick it**.
- **Shadowgreed Eclipse** *(interruptible)* — channels **8 seconds** of powerful party-wide damage.
  **Interrupt it.** Group defensives are the fallback, not the plan.
- **Temptation** — repeatedly pokes random players for minor damage and stacks **Deepening Shadows,
  +1% haste per stack**. This is the fight's soft enrage: the longer you take, the faster everything
  else comes. It is the reason a slow, tidy Bael'Aurum kill still fails.
- **The group stacks together to bait treasure placement** somewhere convenient for the orb path.
- Dodge **Coalescing Avarice** tornados — they block the orb's path, which is the real cost of being
  hit.
- Dodge **Erupting Envy** frontals at random players.

## Boss 3: Deathless Katrine
- **Phase 1:** kill the two **Scuttlejack Cannoneers** immediately, prioritising the **Shackle Blast**
  interrupt.
- **Quick Draw** *(interruptible)* — fires **7 bullets** at random non-tank players. Interrupt it.
- Dodge **Soulpowder Cyclone** — a spin attack that also fires lines applying **Soulpowder Shot**,
  which explodes after a few seconds.
- **Sever Soul** — targets a non-tank and **sends them back to the start of the ship**. Expect the
  run back; do not panic and do not chase them.
- **Thwart the Mutiny** — sends the **entire party** through a gauntlet of souls and beams.
- **No Quarters!** — fills the arena with puddles leaving **a single safe soak zone under the boss**.
  Stack in it.
- **Phase 2 (after she resurrects):** **Touch of Death** debuffs everyone and drops a slowing puddle
  when it expires — place them at the edges.

### The three things to get right
1. **Bael'Aurum is a puzzle, not a damage race.** Everyone should understand the orb-and-treasure
   loop before pulling, or you will spend ten minutes achieving nothing.
2. **Do not over-soak Keelhaul.** Two circles, minimum bodies. Extra soakers stack a DoT on
   themselves for no benefit.
3. **Interrupt Mindrender Barrage every time.** It is the one trash cast here that simply kills people.
