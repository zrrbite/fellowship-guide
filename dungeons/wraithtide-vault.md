# Wraithtide Vault — Capstone (3 bosses)

**Bosses:** First Mate Marrow, Bael'Aurum, Deathless Katrine · **Length:** 28-32 min

**League role:** the **third capstone** — **timing it promotes you to Paragon**
**Drops:** Torment of Bael'Aurum (+4% primary stat, cheat-death heal), plus **exclusive relics**

Pirates and otherworldly enemies. This is the run that gets you into Paragon, which is where
Legendaries realistically start dropping, so it is worth real effort.

## Route

**Head right toward First Mate Marrow first**, then through the jungle to **Bael'Aurum**, and
finally **Deathless Katrine**. The dungeon is a **large loop**, so you meet all the trash regardless
of which way you start — the order is about boss difficulty pacing, not skipping content.

## Trash that kills casual groups

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Greedbound Prism** | **Mindrender Barrage** — heavy damage to the entire group | **Interrupt or CC this channel.** Highest priority in the dungeon. |
| **Greedspawn Sentinel** | **Greed's Embrace** reduces mob damage taken by **80%** | Interrupt it or the pull takes five times as long |
| **Scuttlejack Cannoneer** | **Shackle Blast** (chains) and **Blunder Boom** (bomb circles) | Interrupt or CC |
| **Bilgerat** | **Vermin Tide** spawns Gunpowder Rats that deal heavy area damage | Interrupt the channel immediately |

## Boss 1: First Mate Marrow
- **Wavestomp** — frontal cone with movement and haste reduction. **Tank does not stack with DPS.**
- **Cannonball Troops** spawn adds; run an **interrupt rotation on the Tide Witch**.
- **Keelhaul** — two soak circles. **Stack for them, but do not over-soak**: extra bodies stack a magic DoT.
- **High Tide** intermission — **click the anchor to tether yourself**, then avoid the sharks swimming in lines.

## Boss 2: Bael'Aurum
An unusual fight: **no tanking required**.
- The tank's job is to **push the Orb of Bael'Aurum over Cursed Treasure piles** to damage the boss during the **Hoard** channel.
- **The group stacks together to bait treasure placement** somewhere convenient for the orb path.
- Dodge **Coalescing Avarice** tornados — they block the orb's path, which is the real cost of being hit.
- Dodge **Erupting Envy** frontals.
- **Shadowgreed Eclipse** pulses damage for 8 seconds — group defensives.

## Boss 3: Deathless Katrine
- **Phase 1:** kill the two **Scuttlejack Cannoneers** immediately, prioritising the **Shackle Blast** interrupt.
- Dodge **Soulpowder Cyclone** lines; interrupt **Quick Draw**.
- **No Quarters!** — stack in the safe zone during the puddle phase.
- **Phase 2 (after she resurrects):** manage **Touch of Death** puddle placement and handle **Sever Soul** on random players.

### The three things to get right
1. **Bael'Aurum is a puzzle, not a damage race.** Everyone should understand the orb-and-treasure
   loop before pulling, or you will spend ten minutes achieving nothing.
2. **Do not over-soak Keelhaul.** Two circles, minimum bodies. Extra soakers stack a DoT on
   themselves for no benefit.
3. **Interrupt Mindrender Barrage every time.** It is the one trash cast here that simply kills people.
