# Sailor's Abyss — Adventure (1 boss)

**Boss:** Sinthara · **Length:** 10-15 min · **Difficulty:** A — easy

**Drops:** Haunting Lament (healers), Sinthara's Veil (tanks)

Routing options are limited, but most enemies either pose little threat or pressure only the tank.
A competent tank carries a lot of this dungeon.

## Route

**Non-linear with no good alternate routes.** Keep left on entry, then follow the right-hand path
throughout. There is little decision-making here, which makes it reliable for a weekly group.

## Trash that kills casual groups

Note that all three of the dangerous mobs here are **CC-immune** — do not waste time trying.

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Water Elemental** | **CC-immune.** Healing Waters restores mob health; casts Hydrobolt. | **Interrupt Healing Waters.** Stack in melee to deny Hydrobolt casts. |
| **Deepbound Magi** | **CC-immune.** Shadow Eruption is AoE plus a damage amplifier; also Necrotic Bolt and Harrowing Solitude. | Interrupt Shadow Eruption. Group-soak Harrowing Solitude. |
| **Hollowed Corsair** | **CC-immune.** Melee swings cleave with **Wailing Strike**, applying a stacking bleed. | **Tank faces these away from the group.** Non-tanks stay out of the cleave arc. |

## Boss: Sinthara

Fought on a platform with a **central mast**, which is the key terrain feature.

| Mechanic | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Deafening Screech** | Group damage that **silences casters mid-spell** | Do not start a cast into it |
| **Binding Strike** | Tethers the tank to a random player for 12s, damage scaling with distance | **Group up preemptively** so the tether starts short |
| **Undertow** | DoT on 2 non-tanks, leaving puddles when it expires | Dispel quickly, but **walk the puddles to the platform edge** first — away from the mast |
| **Necrotic Wave** | Targets a player with knockback damage | **Hide behind the central mast** — this avoids damage and knockback entirely |
| **Siren's Song** | At **50% health**, charms players and walks them off the platform | **Interact with the mast** to stay safe while charmed |

### The three things to get right
1. **Use the mast.** It negates Necrotic Wave outright and is your Siren's Song answer. Everyone
   should know where it is before the pull.
2. **Stay grouped for Binding Strike.** Distance is damage, and the tether picks randomly.
3. **Drag Undertow puddles to the edge.** Dispelling in the middle slowly makes the fight unwinnable.
