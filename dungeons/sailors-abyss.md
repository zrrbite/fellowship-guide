# Sailor's Abyss — Adventure (1 boss)

**Boss:** Sinthara · **Length:** 10-15 min · **Difficulty:** A — easy

**Drops:** Haunting Lament (healers), Sinthara's Veil (tanks)

Routing options are limited, but most enemies either pose little threat or pressure only the tank.
A competent tank carries a lot of this dungeon.

## Route

**Non-linear with no good alternate routes.** Keep left on entry, then follow the right-hand path
throughout. There is little decision-making here, which makes it reliable for a weekly group.

## Trash that kills casual groups

Note that all three of the dangerous mobs here are **CC-immune** — do not waste time trying.

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Water Elemental** | **CC-immune.** **Healing Waters** restores mob health; also casts **Hydrobolt**. | **Interrupt Healing Waters** — top priority. Stack in melee to deny Hydrobolt casts. |
| **Deepbound Magi** | **CC-immune.** **Call of the Abyss** (priority interrupt) and **Necrotic Bolt**. Shadow Eruption is AoE plus a damage amplifier; Harrowing Solitude needs a group soak. | Interrupt Call of the Abyss. Group-soak Harrowing Solitude. |
| **Tormented Spectre** | **Anguish Unleashed** — priority interrupt | Interrupt every cast |
| **Hollowed Corsair** | **CC-immune.** Melee swings cleave with **Wailing Strike**, applying a stacking bleed. | **Tank faces these away from the group.** Non-tanks stay out of the cleave arc. |

## Boss: Sinthara

Fought on a platform with a **central mast**. The mast is not scenery — it is the answer to three of
her five abilities.

> **Nothing Sinthara casts is interruptible.** Every kick you have is for the trash on the way in.

| Ability | Recast | Interruptible | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Deafening Screech** | 23s | No | 2s channel, then high damage to everyone **in line of sight**, interrupting their casts | **Break line of sight behind the mast.** Standing in the open and merely holding your cast still eats the damage |
| **Necrotic Wave** *(Vayr's Legacy — Adept+)* | 26s | No | Large frontal at a random player; after 3s, damage and a long knockback | **Hide behind the mast** — it avoids damage and knockback entirely |
| **Undertow** | **30s** | No | Debuff on **2 non-tanks**; on expiry or dispel it leaves a **Watery Grave** that silences and slows | Dispel quickly, but **walk the puddle to the platform edge first**, away from the mast |
| **Binding Strike** | **30s** | No | Tethers the current target to one other random player; DoT scales with the distance between them | **Group up preemptively** so the tether starts short |
| **Siren Song** | at 50% HP | No | Players are silenced and walk uncontrollably toward her | **Interact with the mast** to anchor yourself |

### The 30-second crunch

Undertow and Binding Strike share an identical **30-second** recast, so they arrive together. That
is the moment groups die: two people are tethered and must stay close, while two people are carrying
Watery Graves that must be walked *away* from the mast — and the mast is where everyone needs to be
for the next Deafening Screech at 23s.

Resolve it by rule rather than by improvisation: **tethered pair stays near the mast; Undertow
carriers walk to the edge and come straight back.** Do not let the same player try to do both.

### The three things to get right
1. **Use the mast for three things, not one.** It blocks Deafening Screech line of sight, negates
   Necrotic Wave, and anchors you through Siren Song. Everyone should know where it is before the
   pull.
2. **Stay grouped for Binding Strike.** Distance is damage, and the tether picks randomly.
3. **Drag Undertow puddles to the edge.** Dropping Watery Graves around the mast slowly makes the
   fight unwinnable, because the mast is the one place you cannot afford to lose.
