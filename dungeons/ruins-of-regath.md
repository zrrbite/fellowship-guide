# Ruins of Regath — Adventure (1 boss)

**Boss:** Xurath, Fist of the Warden · **Length:** 10-15 min · **Difficulty:** Easy · **Added:** Season 3

> ## Farm this one
> The second of the two Adventures dropping **Seal of the Heskyr** (+25% Gem Power). Pair it with
> [Scryer's Peak](scryers-peak.md) as the backbone of the weekly farm block.

Flexible. Load a route from the [Method Route Planner](https://www.method.gg/fellowship/route-planner/ruins-of-regath)

## Trash that kills casual groups

Same Heskyr faction as Scryer's Peak, so the interrupt assignments carry over.

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Heskyr Magus** | **Sanguine Maelstrom** — large channelled AoE every second, plus stacking debuffs | Interrupt Sanguine Maelstrom and Blood Bolt; dispel the stacks |
| **Sanguine Disciple** | Channelled **Sanguine Ritual** | Interrupt — weakens the summoned adds |
| **Stalking Grim** | **Piercing Howl** silences any player whose cast finishes during it | Watch for it during critical casts. It can eat a healer's big heal. |
| **Heskyr Zealot** | Buffs nearby allies' damage | Kill early in the pull |

## Boss: Xurath, Fist of the Warden

This is a **staged** fight. It gets harder in three steps and each step is announced.

| Mechanic | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Unholy Hymn** | At **80%, 60% and 40%** HP, Xurath empowers one ability. Rune zones on the floor show which. | **Stand in the correct rune zone.** Wrong positioning is massive damage. This is the mechanic that wipes groups. |
| **Sanguine Overflow** | Pools appear instantly and must be soaked or they explode | Soak immediately. Intensity increases each stage. |
| **Seeping Reliquary** | Escalating party-wide damage across three stages; Stage 3 also slows you | Healer cooldowns planned per stage, not reactively |
| **Seek and Destroy** | Charges a target — later stages add bleeds, then double charges | Expect two charges late in the fight |
| **Crushing Darkness** | Stage-based slam; fires projectiles in later phases | Dodge projectiles once stage 2+ |

### The three things to get right
1. **Learn the Unholy Hymn rune zones.** Three transitions, three chances to wipe. Call them aloud.
2. **Soak every Sanguine Overflow pool** — they are instant, so nobody gets to finish a cast first.
3. **Plan healing by stage.** Seeping Reliquary damage is predictable and escalating, so cooldowns
   should be scheduled rather than reactive.
