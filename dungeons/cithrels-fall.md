# Cithrel's Fall — Capstone (3 bosses)

**Bosses:** Noor the Betrayer, Ancient Koros, Cithrel · **Length:** 28-32 min

**League role:** the **Contender capstone** — clearing it promotes you to Adept
**Drops:** Draconic Might (crits can grant +18% primary stat)

A gauntlet-style dungeon with endless waves of enemies. This is likely the first Capstone your group
will attempt, so treat the first night on it as a learning run rather than a clear attempt.

## Route

1. **Go left** down the humanoid path to **Noor, the Betrayer** — gentler pacing, good warm-up
2. Cross to the elemental side for **Ancient Koros**
3. Progress upward to **Cithrel**

Left-first is the deliberate recommendation: the humanoid trash lets the group settle before the
elemental packs on the right.

## Trash that kills casual groups

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Eldrin Sentinel** | **Shattering Barrier** pulses damage on shielded targets | Interrupt or CC **every** cast |
| **Deceitful Scholar** | Rune of Detonation, Arcane Strike; spawns Volatile Orbs | Interrupt both. **Kite the orbs away from the group.** |
| **Corrupter** | Drain Life, Ice Bolt, and **Freezing Blood** | Interrupt the casts. **Dispel Freezing Blood before it expires** or it deals massively increased damage. |
| **Spellbound Golem** | **CC-immune.** Ice Spike circles; **splits into three Ice Shardlings on death**. | Avoid the circles and be ready for the adds |

> **None of the three bosses has a single interruptible cast.** Every interrupt you bring is for
> the trash. Do not hold kicks "for the boss" — there is nothing to hold them for.

## Boss 1: Noor, the Betrayer
- **Cold-Blooded Twist** — Noor jumps to the arena centre and splits the room into **10 alternating
  safe and icy sections**, firing icicle barrages that rotate **counter-clockwise**. The number of
  waves grows as the fight goes on, up to **5**. Dodge by zone, and learn the rotation direction.
- **Razor Trap** — spawns under a random player and arms after a short delay, **rooting** anyone
  caught. Space them out carefully; badly placed traps block your own dodge lanes later.
- **Betrayer's Arrow** — moderate damage to a random player, then to **anyone near them** after a
  delay. Move away from the group when targeted.
- **Arcane Volley** — applies a **stacking Magic DoT to the entire party**, ticking every second for
  6 seconds. Unavoidable; the healer plans for it.
- **Actuate Ambush** — summons **two Eldrin Recruits and an Eldrin Sentinel**. Interrupt the
  Sentinel's Shattering Barrier.

## Boss 2: Ancient Koros
- **Volatile Orb** — follows the **furthest** player and deals huge group-wide damage on impact.
  **Designate one ranged player to bait it** while everyone else stays near the boss.
- **Shatterstrike** — frontal cone at a random player after a few seconds. It **destroys Volatile
  Orbs**, so the orb baiter's job is to line the orb up with it.
- **Converging Cold** — applied to anyone caught in Shatterstrike's cone: a powerful Magic DoT and
  slow that **freezes the target solid if it expires**. **Dispel it immediately** — this is not
  optional, it is a death.
- **Calamitous Fracture** — creates a rift that must be soaked or it fractures into a massive one.
  The soaker takes the **Calamity Rift** debuff, so **rotate the soak between three players**.

## Boss 3: Cithrel
- **Breath of Al'zerac** — powerful frontal cone at the tank. Point it away and use a defensive.
- **Path to Oblivion** — targets a player with an explosion the party must soak, followed by a
  **second explosion shortly after**. **Stack on the target**, use group damage reduction, and do
  not scatter after the first blast.
- **Bloodhunter's Mark** — marks **two** players, who are then hit by **Bloodhunt**: Cithrel dashes
  between them, dealing AoE damage to each and applying a bleed. Spread the marked pair away from
  everyone else.
- **Maelstrom** — pulls all players toward Cithrel. Expect it while repositioning for Bloodhunt.
- **At 50%, Draconic Frenzy** increases the frequency of **all** his abilities for the rest of the
  fight — **pool cooldowns and burst through it**.

### The three things to get right
1. **Rotate Calamitous Fracture soaks on Koros.** One person soaking everything dies.
2. **Save cooldowns for Cithrel's 50% mark.** Draconic Frenzy is a soft enrage; burning cooldowns
   early is how groups lose a 25-minute run at the end.
3. **Dispel Freezing Blood in the trash.** It is the biggest avoidable damage spike before the bosses.
