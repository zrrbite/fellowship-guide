# Cithrel's Fall — Capstone (3 bosses)

**Bosses:** Noor the Betrayer, Ancient Koros, Cithrel · **Length:** 28-32 min

**League role:** the **Contender capstone** — clearing it promotes you to Adept
**Drops:** Draconic Might (crits can grant +18% primary stat)

A gauntlet-style dungeon with endless waves of enemies. This is likely the first Capstone your group
will attempt, so treat the first night on it as a learning run rather than a clear attempt.

## Route

1. **Go left** down the humanoid path to **Noor, the Betrayer** — gentler pacing, good warm-up
2. Cross to the elemental side for **Ancient Koros**
3. Progress upward to **Cithrel**

Left-first is the deliberate recommendation: the humanoid trash lets the group settle before the
elemental packs on the right.

## Trash that kills casual groups

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Eldrin Sentinel** | **Shattering Barrier** pulses damage on shielded targets | Interrupt or CC **every** cast |
| **Deceitful Scholar** | Rune of Detonation, Arcane Strike; spawns Volatile Orbs | Interrupt both. **Kite the orbs away from the group.** |
| **Corrupter** | Drain Life, Ice Bolt, and **Freezing Blood** | Interrupt the casts. **Dispel Freezing Blood before it expires** or it deals massively increased damage. |
| **Spellbound Golem** | **CC-immune.** Ice Spike circles; **splits into three Ice Shardlings on death**. | Avoid the circles and be ready for the adds |

## Boss 1: Noor, the Betrayer
- **Cold-Blooded Twist** — alternating safe and frozen zones with icicles firing in increasing waves. Dodge by zone.
- **Razor Traps** — space them out carefully; badly placed traps block your own dodge lanes later.
- **Betrayer's Arrow** — move away from the group when targeted.
- Adds are **Eldrin Sentinels** — interrupt Shattering Barrier.

## Boss 2: Ancient Koros
- **Volatile Orbs** — **designate one ranged player to bait them** while everyone else stays near the boss.
- Destroy orbs with **Shatterstrike's frontal cone**, then **dispel Converging Cold immediately**.
- **Calamitous Fracture** — **rotate the soak between three players**; the arena accumulates hazards, so spreading the assignment matters.

## Boss 3: Cithrel
- **Breath of Al'zerac** — tank points it away and uses a defensive.
- **Path to Oblivion** — **stack on the target** and use group damage reduction.
- **Bloodhunter's Mark** — spread away from allies; watch Maelstrom pulls while repositioning.
- **At 50%, Draconic Frenzy** speeds up his ability rotation — **pool cooldowns and burst through it**.

### The three things to get right
1. **Rotate Calamitous Fracture soaks on Koros.** One person soaking everything dies.
2. **Save cooldowns for Cithrel's 50% mark.** Draconic Frenzy is a soft enrage; burning cooldowns
   early is how groups lose a 25-minute run at the end.
3. **Dispel Freezing Blood in the trash.** It is the biggest avoidable damage spike before the bosses.
