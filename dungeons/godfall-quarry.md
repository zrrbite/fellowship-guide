# Godfall Quarry — Adventure

**Boss:** Godfall Titan · **Length:** 10-15 min · **Difficulty:** B

**Drops:** Draconic Deceit (DPS/healer), Draconic Fury (tank)

A magma cavern with a massive titan. The trash is forgiving and there are multiple routing options.
The difficulty is entirely in the boss, specifically in how fast you kill Wardstones.

## Route

**Head down the right path.** You meet the same trash types in different combinations either way.

## Trash that kills casual groups

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Godfall Wardstone** | Casts **Searing Beam** | Interrupt or CC it. **Wardstones are Brittle and become Vulnerable when interrupted or CCd** — so the interrupt is also a damage buff. |
| **Enforcer** | **CC-immune.** Piercing Strike cuts tank armor; **Reflective Shield** reflects damage. | **Stop attacking and move away during Reflective Shield.** |
| **Enraged Earth Elemental** | **CC-immune.** **Shatter** stacks damage amplification. | Defensive cooldowns as stacks climb |
| **Quarry Stalker** | Teleports to a random player and casts **Poisonous Strike** | Interrupt or CC, then dispel the poison |
| **Nezari Bolter** | **Bolt Barrage** | Interrupt |
| **Arcane Warden** | **Psionic Horror** | Interrupt |

## Boss: Godfall Titan (with Vaerith Vorn)

**One encounter, two sources of damage.** When you pull the Titan, **Vaerith Vorn flies off and
attacks from the air** for the rest of the fight. He is not a separate boss and **he cannot be
killed** — every Mana Bomb and Arcane Surge comes from him, and the only way to make them stop is to
kill the Titan. Groups waste time looking for something to target. There is nothing.

### Godfall Titan — on the ground

| Mechanic | Interruptible | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Titanic Slam** | No | Prepares to slam **two locations**. If both are not soaked, huge party-wide damage | **Tank solos one circle, the other three cover the second.** Decide this before the pull |
| **Wardstones** *(Vayr's Legacy — Adept+)* | — | Summons **two Godfall Wardstones** to aid him | Interrupt or CC their **Searing Beam**, which makes them **Vulnerable**, then destroy them |

### Vaerith Vorn — from the air

| Mechanic | Interruptible | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Mana Bomb** | No | After a **Volatile Energy** debuff expires, a bomb appears and detonates, dealing **more damage the closer you are** | Debuffed player **runs out, gets dispelled, comes back** |
| **Arcane Surge** | No | Beams fired **across and along** the platform | Read the directions and move through the gaps |
| **Magic Missiles** | No | Randomly targets players for moderate damage | Chip damage; the healer absorbs it |

### The three things to get right
1. **Kill Wardstones fast.** This is the stated difficulty of the fight: slow Wardstone kills are
   where the damage comes from. Interrupting a Wardstone's Searing Beam makes it **Vulnerable**, so
   the kick is also a damage buff — always kick before you burn it.
2. **Pre-assign the Titanic Slam soaks.** Tank takes one, everyone else takes the other. No debate
   mid-fight.
3. **Run out with Mana Bomb.** The instinct to stay near the healer for the dispel is the wrong one.
