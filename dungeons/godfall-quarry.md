# Godfall Quarry — Adventure

**Boss:** Godfall Titan · **Length:** 10-15 min · **Difficulty:** B

**Drops:** Draconic Deceit (DPS/healer), Draconic Fury (tank)

A magma cavern with a massive titan. The trash is forgiving and there are multiple routing options.
The difficulty is entirely in the boss, specifically in how fast you kill waystones.

## Route

**Head down the right path.** You meet the same trash types in different combinations either way.

## Trash that kills casual groups

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Godfall Wardstone** | Casts **Searing Beam** | Interrupt or CC it. **Wardstones are Brittle and become Vulnerable when interrupted or CCd** — so the interrupt is also a damage buff. |
| **Enforcer** | **CC-immune.** Piercing Strike cuts tank armor; **Reflective Shield** reflects damage. | **Stop attacking and move away during Reflective Shield.** |
| **Enraged Earth Elemental** | **CC-immune.** **Shatter** stacks damage amplification. | Defensive cooldowns as stacks climb |
| **Quarry Stalker** | Teleports to a random player and casts **Poisonous Strike** | Interrupt or CC, then dispel the poison |

## Boss: Godfall Titan

| Mechanic | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Titanic Slam** | Two circles appear, each needing at least one player | **Tank solos one circle, the other three cover the second.** Decide this before the pull. |
| **Mana Bomb** | After Volatile Energy expires a bomb appears and detonates | Debuffed player **runs out, gets dispelled, comes back** |
| **Arcane Surge** | Three beams sweep the platform | Read the directions and move through the gaps |
| **Wardstones** (Vayr's Legacy) | Summons appear on the far side of the arena | Interrupt or CC their casts, then **destroy them while Vulnerable**. Watch for overlap with Mana Bomb. |

### The three things to get right
1. **Kill waystones fast.** This is the stated difficulty of the fight: slow waystone kills is where
   the damage comes from.
2. **Pre-assign the Titanic Slam soaks.** Tank takes one, everyone else takes the other. No debate
   mid-fight.
3. **Run out with Mana Bomb.** The instinct to stay near the healer for the dispel is the wrong one.
