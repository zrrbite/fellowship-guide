# Empyrean Sands — Adventure (1 boss)

**Boss:** Sin-Magir · **Length:** 10-15 min · **Difficulty:** A — easy

**Drops:** Haunting Lament (healers), Sinthara's Veil (tanks)

Desert-themed, with challenging mob patrols. Flexible routing lets you avoid dangerous packs while
still meeting the Kill Score. Healing here was recently retuned to be more manageable.

## Route

Flexible by design — routing around the dangerous packs is the point. Load a route from the
[Method Route Planner](https://www.method.gg/fellowship/route-planner) and watch for patrols, which
are the main thing that turns a planned pull into an unplanned one.

## Trash that kills casual groups

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Greedspawn Sentinel** | **Greed's Embrace** applies a stacking DoT and haste buff to nearby enemies, and reduces mob damage taken by **80%** | **Interrupt or CC immediately.** Top priority in the dungeon. |
| **Gravebreaker Brute** | **CC-immune.** Splash damage to anyone within **4 yards of the tank**. | Ranged players keep their distance from the tank |
| **Nightbound Magi** | **CC-immune.** **Constellation of Ruin** demands everyone spread and soak a separate circle. Also casts **Shadow Bolt** (interruptible, 2s cast). | **Spread and soak one circle each** or eat heavy group damage |
| **Acolyte of Sinnari** | **Sinnari's Wrath** — 3s cast, then a periodic-damage self-buff | Interrupt it |
| **Water Elemental** | **Healing Waters** restores mob health; also **Hydrobolt** | **Interrupt Healing Waters** — top trash priority alongside Greed's Embrace |
| **Dunesnare Hunter** | **Soulpiercer** — **dispellable, not interruptible** | Dispel it. Do not waste a kick trying. |

## Boss: Sin-Magir

> **Nothing Sin-Magir casts is interruptible.** Bank your kicks for the trash. This fight is pure
> execution, and the only clock that matters is Ruinous Starfall's.

| Ability | Recast | Interruptible | What happens | What to do |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Lunar Beam** *(Vayr's Legacy — Adept+)* | **18.5s** | No | Two beams cross the arena, positioned by the **Moon Sigils**; lethal damage | Pre-position using the sundial indicators. **Shortest cycle in the fight** |
| **Ruinous Starfall** | 23s | No | Summons **10 Ruinous Star orbs**, each on a long unstunnable cast; survivors detonate | **Destroy stars before their cast finishes.** Early destruction triggers the damage sooner, grants a **5% group damage buff**, and gives the healer recovery time |
| **Lunar Soulsear** | 28s | No | Large frontal beam at the **highest-aggro** player, channelled 3s | Tank points it away from the group and uses a defensive |
| **Darkstar Ruin** | 30s | No | Summons **Celestial Bursts** raining across the battlefield | Keep moving |
| **Penumbra** | **60s** | No | A sequence of **3 explosions**, the first centred on the boss | **Stand near the boss** — it is easy to dodge from close range |

### Why the fight tightens at Champion

Ruinous Starfall recasts every 23 seconds and Lunar Beam every 18.5 seconds. Once Vayr's Legacy is
active — which it is from Adept onward, so **it is always on for you** — those two cycles overlap
constantly, and orbs must be broken *while* dodging beams. That is the whole difficulty increase.
Assign a **star-breaking priority** so nobody stops DPS on orbs to dodge something avoidable.

### The three things to get right
1. **Ruinous Starfall is the fight.** Break the stars fast. The tank can help by aiming Lunar
   Soulsear beams to cleave nearby stars.
2. **Stack near the boss for Penumbra** — the instinct to run away makes it harder.
3. **Spread for Nightbound Magi in the trash.** Four separate circles, four separate people.

> **Naming note:** older notes in this repo called Lunar Beam *Sinnari's Judgment* and Darkstar Ruin
> *Celestial Burst*. Celestial Bursts are the falling rocks Darkstar Ruin summons, not the cast
> itself. Source naming is used above.
