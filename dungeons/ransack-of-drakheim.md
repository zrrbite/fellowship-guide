# Ransack of Drakheim — Capstone (3 bosses)

**Bosses:** Fodir Kaldur, Chilgar the Drowned, Auga Handhafi the Blind · **Length:** 28-32 min

**Difficulty:** the hardest Capstone — an endurance run
**Drops:** Drakheim's Absolution (Spirit Ability grants +20% primary stat)

An orc invasion scenario with interconnected trash areas. After the first boss you choose between
two paths to the remaining two.

## Route

The PUG-friendly order, and the right one for a group still learning:

1. Enter and clear the **right side** trash toward Fodir Kaldur
2. **Kill both mini-bosses** (see below — this is not optional)
3. Fodir Kaldur
4. Head **left** into the icy area for Chilgar's trash
5. Chilgar the Drowned
6. Auga Handhafi the Blind

**Kill both mini-bosses before Fodir Kaldur.** Doing so gives the boss a **10% damage increase
debuff instead of his aura buffs** — a straight trade in your favour.

- **Kaldurson Eldsupptok** (fire) — **Burning Aura** applies a stacking magic DoT **when you move**
- **Kaldurson Isilagður** (ice) — **Freezing Aura** applies a stacking DoT and slow **when you stand still**

## Trash that kills casual groups

| Mob | What it does | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| **Icebite Reaver** | **CC-immune.** **Creeping Frostbite** stacks **+5% damage taken per stack**. | **Interrupt Frostbitten Strength.** The stacks are what kill the tank. |
| **Bleakfrost Mystic** | **Invigorate** heals mobs; **Eye of Ul'morgat** rotating beams | Interrupt the heal; dodge the beams |
| **Pillager** | Gains stacking buffs when nearby enemies die | **Cleave evenly** so nothing dies early |
| **Ashen Arsonist** | **Big Oogha Flame** (interruptible DoT), **Flame Blast** (frontal cone) | Interrupt; stay out of the cone |
| **Craven Hiisi** | Leaps and channels **Bonk Noggin** | **CC** to stop it |
| **Frenzied Looter** | Drops gold piles giving a damage buff but applying a slow | Judgement call — the slow is often not worth it |
| **Bleakfrost Slaver** | **Barched Chain** links players | Interrupt; **linked players stand together** |
| **Nightmist Whisperer** | **Blinding Fear**, plus a Silence | Interrupt Fear; CC or dispel the Silence |

## Boss 1: Fodir Kaldur
The aura fight. He alternates **Burning Aura** (damage when you move) and **Freezing Aura** (damage
when you stand still).
- **Position at the room edges and rotate counter-clockwise**, overlapping puddles so the centre stays open.
- **Blazing Slam** leaves **permanent** puddles plus damage circles — this is why the rotation matters.
- **Ice Tomb** stuns a random non-tank until destroyed. **Break it fast** — stand in puddles for bonus damage.
- **Frost Bite** is a tank DoT — defensives ready.
- Healers dispel stacks for anyone who cannot move well.

## Boss 2: Chilgar the Drowned
Pure positioning and awareness. Constant movement.
- **Freezing Flood** waves sweep the arena applying a DoT and slow.
- **Arctic Winds** tornadoes spiral outward with knockback.
- **Glacial Slam** — **tank stands away from the group**.
- **Grasping Pillar** spawns under the **furthest player** and erupts. **Designate one player to bait
  pillars to the arena edge**, keeping the centre clear. Only destroy pillars if people get chained.

## Boss 3: Auga Handhafi the Blind
A dance fight with a DPS-check intermission.
- **Revelation** roots a random player and creates a group soak — **start phases stacked**.
- **Shadow Conduit** puts two lines in a narrow X through all players — **then loosely spread**.
- **Dark Ritual** summons four **Nightmist Fiends**. **Find the Fiend whose symbol does not match the
  three arena totems and focus it.**
- **From the second intermission onward**, Shadow Conduit lines appear *during* the add phase,
  rotating through players. This is the hardest moment in the dungeon.

### The three things to get right
1. **Kill the mini-bosses first.** A free 10% damage increase on Fodir Kaldur, and it removes his aura buffs.
2. **Learn the aura rule and say it out loud each swap** — "moving hurts" / "standing hurts". Groups
   lose this fight to confusion, not damage.
3. **One dedicated pillar baiter on Chilgar.** Without one, the arena closes and everyone dies.
