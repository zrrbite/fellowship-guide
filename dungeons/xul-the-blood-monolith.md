# Xul, the Blood Monolith — Pinnacle Dungeon

**Bosses:** Xul'vorith Sentinel of the Bloodbound Gate, Han-Eth Sentry of Xul,
Vel'korath the Sanctified Vision of the Covenant

**Unlocks at:** **Eternal 5** · **Difficulties:** Normal, Hard, Nightmare · **Added:** Season 3

> **No timer.** Unlike every other dungeon in the game, the clock never judges you — wipe as many
> times as your dignity allows. For a casual group this is significant: it is the one piece of
> endgame content where our lack of practice costs nothing but time.

## Why it is a weekly objective

| Reward | Detail |
|---|---|
| **First clear each week** | Grants **5x** the gold, materials and gear |
| **Bloodstones** | Once weekly **per difficulty**. Upgrade gear to **Regal, item level 315**, preserving existing modifiers. |
| **Tempering bonus** | **3x on Normal, 5x on Hard, 8x on Nightmare** |
| **Mounts** | Auxilian Prowler (Normal), Auxilian Matriarch (Hard), Auxilian Slayer (Nightmare) |

Repeat runs give diminishing returns, so the correct pattern is **one clear per difficulty per
week**, not farming.

## Boss 1: Xul'vorith, Sentinel of the Bloodbound Gate

A tank-driven encounter built on **sequential edict phases**. **Nothing he casts is interruptible.**

| Ability | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Exsanguinating Strike** | Medium tank damage with a stacking **+10% damage taken** debuff. **Every third strike is enhanced** — the tank plans defensives on a count of three |
| **Nullification** | Frontal cone at the tank that **disables any conduit it strikes**. This is the tank's tool, not just a hazard |
| **Gatekeeper's Judgment** | At **70% and 50%**: pulls everyone in, deals AoE damage, **resets the conduits** and applies a random Edict |
| **Gatekeeper's Edict** | One of three rotating debuffs: **Frailty** (−20% healing), **Burden** (−30% movement speed), or **Isolation** (10% max HP damage to nearby allies — spread immediately) |
| **Conduit of the Bleeding Gaze** | Summons eyes that fire projectiles |
| **Conduit of the Burning Circle** | Magic DoT on a player that **explodes on dispel or expiry** |
| **Conduit of Striking Shadows** | Party-wide ticking damage **every 1.5 seconds** |

- Three **conduits** sit around the arena. In each edict phase the tank must **take the boss to a
  conduit and aim Nullification at it** to deactivate it.
- **Deactivating a conduit is a trade**: the source states it grants Xul'vorith **+20% damage**. So
  conduits are not simply "clear them all as fast as possible" — killing pressure you can heal
  through in exchange for a boss that hits harder is not always correct. *(The direction of this
  trade is the one figure in this page worth verifying yourself on a Normal run.)*
- **On Hard, Xul'vorith gains no new mechanics** — only more health and damage. If you can do it on
  Normal, you can do it on Hard with better gear.
- **On Nightmare, two edicts activate simultaneously each phase**, in rotating combinations.

**This fight lives or dies on the tank.** Everyone else's job is to stay out of the cone and keep
spread discipline.

## Boss 2: Han-Eth, Sentry of Xul

A soak-and-adds fight. **Nothing he casts is interruptible** — but the adds he makes are another
matter.

| Ability | What happens |
|---|---|
| **Palace Response Unit** | Waves, in order: **1 Abomination → 2 Broods → 4 Broods → 4 Broods + 1 Abomination**. **Han-Eth is invulnerable while any Abomination lives** |
| **Summon Creations (Clots)** | **12 small soak pools**. Any left unsoaked spawn Clot Spawns |
| **Summon Creations (Greater Clots)** | **3 large pools requiring a 3-second stand**. Unsoaked ones spawn Greater Clot Spawns |
| **Draw Blood** | Sucks in the Clot Spawns and **detonates them** for party damage — this is the bill for every pool you missed |
| **Vein Seeds** | Roughly every **15s**: orbs fan outward, stacking **Vein Sprout**. **3 stacks roots you in vines** |
| **Vein Constriction** | Movement slow plus a DoT |
| **Annihilation Protocol** | A rotating beam sweeping the arena for massive damage on contact |
| **Judgement** | At **25%**: party-wide damage every 3 seconds, **increasing 2% per tick**. A hard enrage — this is your burn window |

- **Killing the Abomination first is mandatory**, not optional — damage on the boss while an
  Abomination lives is wasted entirely.
- **Soak discipline is the fight.** Draw Blood turns every missed pool into party damage later, so
  the twelve small pools matter more than they look.
- **On Hard**, a dispellable magic debuff is added (orange circles), and beam contact deals party
  damage that will usually wipe you.
- **On Nightmare**, a **Heart of Rot and 2 Sanguine Echos** spawn after the first Abomination, and
  the beams stay **permanently active** through the burn phase.

## Boss 3: Vel'korath the Sanctified, Vision of the Covenant

The final encounter — and, unusually for this dungeon, **the most interrupt-dependent fight in the
game**. Three of his casts are kickable, and two of those are party-wide damage.

| Ability | Interruptible | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| **Tormenting Visions** | **Yes** | Channel pulsing heavy damage around the tank |
| **Sinister Vessels** | **Yes** | Roughly every **25s**: illusions fire a wide beam at a targeted player. **Double damage if you are hit by an ally's beam too** — so never line up behind each other |
| **Hungering Vessels** | **Yes** | Roughly every **40s**: illusions channel party-wide damage every 2 seconds |
| **Contrite** | No | Tank slam with a stacking 6-second debuff, **+20% damage taken per stack** |
| **Marching Vessels** | No | Roughly every **15s**: a wave of blood adds applying a high-damage DoT every 2 seconds |
| **Truesight Corruption** | No | Taken deliberately from a bowl. Grants **True Sight** to see the illusions, at the cost of a DoT every 2 seconds. **Dispellable** — and lost once the maze ends |
| **One True Path** | No | Maze intermission at **70% and 50%**, on a **30-second timer**, with a **different path for each player** |
| **Repent** | No | The failure penalty if the maze is not completed: high damage every 3 seconds for 20 seconds |
| **Blood Sermon** | No | At **25%**: three runes spawn; each player must stand in the correct one for the symbol shown |
| **Apotheosis** | No | At **25%**: exploding puddles spawn at random |

**How to approach it:**
1. **Assign the three interrupts before you pull.** Hungering Vessels at ~40s and Sinister Vessels at
   ~25s drift against each other, so they will eventually land together — the same convergence
   problem as [Wyrmheart](wyrmheart.md), with higher stakes.
2. **Someone must take Truesight Corruption** to see the illusions, and accept the DoT. Decide who,
   and decide whether the healer dispels it early or rides it out.
3. **The maze is individual.** Nobody can call it for anyone else; each player learns their own path.
   Thirty seconds is generous the third time and brutal the first.
4. **On Hard**, the tank accumulates **Tired Eyes** from Truesight Corruption (+1% damage taken per
   stack) and beam hits leave **Lingering Eradication**, an 8-second dispellable DoT.
5. **On Nightmare**, **Blood Orbs** spawn from the last-used bowl and heal Vel'korath **5%** if they
   reach him — and they persist through the maze phases, when nobody is watching them.

## How to approach it as a casual group

1. **Do not attempt it before Eternal 5.** It is gated, and rightly.
2. **Run Normal first, every week, for the 5x first clear.** That alone justifies the twenty minutes.
3. **Add Hard once Normal is routine.** Xul'vorith gains nothing but numbers, so it is a gear check
   rather than a new fight to learn.
4. **Treat Nightmare as a season-long stretch goal.** Simultaneous edicts and instant punishment for
   clustering is a big step up from what a weekly group typically drills.
