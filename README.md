# Fellowship — A Guide for a Once-a-Week Group

A practical progression guide for **casual players who raid one night a week**.
Current for **Early Access Season 3 — "Rise of the Heskyr"**.

Most Fellowship guides are written for people playing twenty hours a week. This one is not. It
assumes you get **one session**, that half the group forgets the route between weeks, and that
nobody is respeccing between pulls. Everything here is filtered through one question: **what gives
the most progress per hour of actual play?**

## The three things that matter most

1. **Always run with the same four people.** **Matchmaker's Boon** gives a stable party **+25% gold
   and materials** plus increased gem and crafting drop chances. A fixed weekly group gets the
   single best farming buff in the game for free, simply by existing. Do not pug.
2. **Farm Scryer's Peak and Ruins of Regath.** They are the only Adventure dungeons that drop
   **Seal of the Heskyr** (+25% Gem Power), which is best-in-slot for all twelve heroes. They are
   also rated easy. This is the highest-value farm in the game for a group like ours.
3. **Take the higher item level piece. Every time.** Until deep Eternal, main stat drives damage,
   healing *and* survivability at once. Stat optimisation is a problem for people with more hours
   than us.

Vigour (Healer — reactive, forgiving), Meiko or Helena (Tank). **Hardest:** Tariq (swing timer),
Sylvie (proactive pet healing), Gunde (three divergent builds).
