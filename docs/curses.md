# Dungeon Curses

Curses are run-wide modifiers, separate from [Vayr's Legacy](dungeons.md#vayrs-legacy) boss
abilities. **They begin at Adept, and Champion unlocks four more.** Read the curse before you pull —
several of them change which mechanics are survivable, not just how much damage you take.

Every curse also carries a **bonus**: a reward for engaging with it rather than enduring it. For a
once-a-week group the bonuses are usually worth more than the safety of ignoring them, because they
compound across a whole run.

## Curses available from Adept

| Curse | What it does | The bonus worth chasing |
|---|---|---|
| **Binding Ice** | One hero is periodically cursed; after **5 seconds** it erupts, damaging all heroes in the radius and **rooting them for 3 seconds** | Enemies take the damage too — so **walk it into the pack** before it goes off |
| **Blood Shards** | Dying enemies erupt for **2% of your maximum health** | Heroes hit gain **+1% Expertise for 30s, stacking to 15**. Standing in shards is correct |
| **Anomalous Orbs** | Three orbs spawn near enemies periodically and cast **Detonate — which is interruptible** | Destroying an orb grants **+480 Gem Power** of its colour. Free gem progress every pull |
| **Empowered Minions** | Several enemies per dungeon gain **+100% health and +150% Power** | Killing one grants **+20% Haste and +20% Movement Speed for 40 seconds**. Pull them *into* your next pack |
| **Meteor Rain** | Burning meteors fall on heroes, splitting into fragments that damage and **stun** | Heroes generate **2 Spirit** toward their Spirit Ability |

## Curses that unlock at Champion

These are the ones our group has not seen before. All four are meaningfully harder than the Adept set.

| Curse | What it does | The bonus worth chasing |
|---|---|---|
| **Ultimatum** | Bosses deal **5% more damage** and have **10% more health** | Hero **cooldowns reduced by 10%** — including your interrupts |
| **Stone Skin** | Non-boss enemies have **+30% maximum health** | They **explode on death for 15% of their max health** as physical damage — which is free AoE if you are stacked, and a wipe if the healer is not ready |
| **Storm Shield** | Players periodically charge with red lightning for **8 seconds**, then a bolt strikes **all players for lethal damage** | Survivors gain **+20% critical strike chance** |
| **Shadow Lord's Trial** | **Kill score does not accrue normally.** Enemies drop Shadow Orbs instead; an Emissary spawns after you collect **30** | Defeating the Emissary grants **+20% damage, Haste and Movement Speed** |

## Optional bosses

Not curses exactly, but they appear by the same mechanism and both give back clock:

| Encounter | Reward |
|---|---|
| **Ghorn the Avalanche** | **Ghorn's Boon** and **+20 seconds** to the dungeon timer. He then assists you in boss fights |
| **Eira the White Witch** | **+90 seconds** to the dungeon timer. She spawns Winter Runes granting shields and healing |

**Eira is worth a detour on any timed run.** Ninety seconds is a large fraction of the margin on a
Champion +4.

## True Sight — new in Season 3

At **30%, 55% and 80% kill score**, orbs drop that reveal hidden enemies. Defeating a marked enemy
grants **+20% Damage and +20% Healing for 50 seconds**. Plan to cash these in on the next big pull
rather than wandering into one by accident.

## Which curses actually change our tactics

**Ultimatum makes every DPS race worse.** It is the curse to fear in
[Wyrmheart](../dungeons/wyrmheart.md), where the fight is already a race against permanent floor
loss — 10% more boss health is 10% more Frigid Mists casts to interrupt. The 10% cooldown reduction
partly repays you, since it also applies to your kicks.

**Anomalous Orbs competes for interrupts.** Detonate is interruptible, so on a boss like Apostate
Veras or Warlord Brogg you now have a third thing asking for kicks. **The banked interrupt stays
banked** — orbs are a bonus, Frigid Mists is the fight.

**Storm Shield fights against every spread mechanic.** Lethal damage to all players means group
cooldowns on a timer, which is awkward in exactly the fights that force you apart —
Glacial Spikes, Crystal Volley, Bloodmarked Assault.

**Stone Skin makes big pulls better, not worse**, provided the healer knows the explosion is coming.
The death explosions chain through a stacked pack.
