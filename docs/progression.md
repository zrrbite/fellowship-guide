# Progression — Contender to Eternal

This is the spine of the guide. Every hero page references the stages defined here.

## The league ladder

Four Challenger Leagues, then an uncapped endgame:

| League | Difficulties | Unlocks at this tier | Loot rarities |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Contender** | +1 → capstone | Baseline. Learn routes and kits. | Uncommon, Rare |
| **Adept** | +1 → capstone | **Dungeon curses** begin appearing | + Epic |
| **Champion** | +1 → capstone | **Timed runs** at +4; Legendary drop chance opens; weapon traits unlock | + Heroic |
| **Paragon** | +1 → capstone | Expanded boss ability sets | + Regal, Legendary |
| **Eternal** | Infinite scaling | Unlocked by clearing the Paragon capstone | All rarities; socket/legendary odds climb with depth |

> **Season 3 change:** leagues were simplified to 3 difficulty levels per league (down from 6), and
> item level progression per dungeon was raised so the climb feels steadier.

## Item level targets

Hit the *entry* number before you push the next league. Do not grind past the *exit* number in a
league — the loot is capped and your time is better spent one tier up.

| Stage | Enter with | Leave around | Rarity ceiling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Contender | 5–35 (Quartermaster tokens are ilvl 35) | 120–130 | Uncommon 120 |
| Adept | ~130 | 180–190 | Rare 180 |
| Champion | ~190 | 240–250 | Epic 240 |
| Paragon | ~250 | 255–270 | Legendary 255+ |
| Eternal | ~270 | **330 hard cap** on character ilvl | Dropped items top out ~270; upgrades carry you to 330 |

## What to chase, in order

### 1. Contender — quantity over quality
- Claim Quartermaster token gear (ilvl 35) immediately; it is free and it is a floor.
- Spam **Quickplay** for Supplies and Marks of Fellowship.
- Upgrade with **gold only**. Do not burn crafting materials at this stage — they are worthless
  spent on gear you will replace within the hour.
- Equip the higher item level piece every single time. Ignore stats entirely.

### 2. Adept → Champion — start shaping the character
- Farm **capstone dungeons** for set pieces and currency.
- Begin collecting **one relic of each type**; relics occupy 2 gear slots and provide utility
  (damage reduction, crowd control, mobility, teleports, stealth).
- Still: *ilvl is king*. A 190 piece with wrong stats beats a 170 piece with perfect stats.
- At Champion+, **weapon traits unlock**. Note that unlocking *all* weapon trait nodes requires a
  300 ilvl weapon — that is a late Eternal goal, not a Champion one.
- Begin **Arcane Reforging** searches for a best-in-slot weapon at Champion and above.

### 3. Paragon — the Legendary window
- Legendary drop chance opens at Champion but in practice your first Legendary lands in Paragon.
- Your hero's build **branches on which Legendary you get**. Every hero page lists the build
  variants per Legendary — do not force a build for a Legendary you do not own.
- Around **12 talent points** is where most players acquire their Legendary and transition from the
  generic build to the specialised one.

### 4. Eternal — optimisation
Endgame checklist, in priority order:

1. **BiS Legendary + BiS weapon**
2. **Two sets, 4 pieces total** — one of them should be Seal of the Heskyr
3. **Correctly statted gear** (now, finally, stats matter more than raw ilvl)
4. **As many gem sockets as possible**, then colour-optimise via Imbued Transmutation
5. Upgrades become the primary ilvl driver; keep Artisan Supply Box rotations running via Quickplay

## Alt / second hero strategy

Farm the **135 ilvl token set** through Quickplay, then run Cithrel's Fall and Heart of Tuzari to
catch up quickly. **Master Craftsman Braggi** converts account-wide resources into small gems for
alts, so gem progression is not fully restarted per character.

## Talent point acquisition

All **14 points** come from dungeon completions and rating progression, tracked on the **Starmap**
across three progression paths (which also award gear, currency and cosmetics). Respec is free
between runs — retune for single-target vs. AoE dungeons rather than committing to one build.
