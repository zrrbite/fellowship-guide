# Dungeons — Index, Difficulty and Routing

## How dungeons work

- **Adventures** — 10-15 minutes, **1 boss**. The efficient farm for a time-limited group.
- **Capstones** — 28-32 minutes, **3 bosses**. Gate league progression; source of several sets.
- **Pinnacle** — Xul, the Blood Monolith. Unlocks at **Eternal 5**. No timer. Weekly objective.

**You do not clear everything.** You kill enemies until you reach a **Kill Score of 100%**. This is
why routing exists: which trash you skip is a real decision, and it is where most of the time in a
run is won or lost. (Heart of Tuzari, for reference, requires a Kill Score of 201 within a 28-minute
timer.)

**Method's [Route Planner](https://www.method.gg/fellowship/route-planner)** has pre-built routes
for every dungeon at multiple difficulties, and lets you build your own by selecting enemies into
pulls. For a weekly group, load a suggested route once and stick to it — consistency beats optimality.

## Pinnacle

[**Xul, the Blood Monolith**](../dungeons/xul-the-blood-monolith.md) — Normal, Hard and Nightmare.
No timer, so wipes cost only time. **First clear each week grants 5x rewards**; Bloodstones drop once
weekly per difficulty and upgrade gear to Regal ilvl 315. Treat it as a weekly objective.

## Universal pull principles

These apply everywhere and are worth more to a casual group than any specific route.

**Building a pull**
- **Count the interrupts.** Never take a pull needing more simultaneous interrupts than you have
  people paying attention. This is the number one cause of casual wipes.
- **Front-load.** Take the big pull when cooldowns are fresh. Align large pulls with ultimates
  coming back up.
- **Respect the tank.** Mobs that stack damage on the tank (Pillagers and their kin) are the limiter
  on pull size, not raw mob count.
- **Watch instant-cast group damage.** Several small pulls of it will overwhelm a healer faster than
  one big pull of avoidable damage.

**Relics are underused.** They are a source of genuinely creative play: **Chicken** a dangerous
elite to simplify a pull, **Shroud** (stealth) past the two worst packs in a dungeon, use portals to
skip backtracking. Method's routes treat relics as essential from Paragon upward. Most casual groups
carry them and never press them.

**Grow into it.** Start with small pulls. Higher difficulty routes use fewer, larger pulls to beat
the timer — that is an optimisation to adopt once the mechanics are automatic, not before.

**Mobs immune to CC** appear in nearly every dungeon and are flagged on each page. Knowing which
ones cannot be controlled prevents the most demoralising failure mode: pressing your CC and watching
nothing happen.

## Vayr's Legacy

Several dungeon pages reference **Vayr's Legacy** mechanics. These are affix-gated additions that
appear at higher difficulty. If a mechanic on a page does not match what you see, you are likely
below the difficulty that enables it.
