# Optimisation — Gems, Reforging, and Reaching the Best Configuration

How to actually get the gear you want, rather than waiting for it to drop. This is the crafting and
economy layer, and it is where a group that plays a few hours a week can close a lot of the gap on
people who play twenty.

**The one-sentence version:** hoard materials until Champion, buy your weapon with Arcane Reforging,
get Seal of the Heskyr **before** optimising gem colours, and never delete a gem.

## The workshop — who does what

Everything lives in your Stronghold.

| NPC / station | Does | Costs |
|---|---|---|
| **Scrapper** | Destroys unwanted gear into **gold and crafting materials**. Shows expected returns before you confirm. | Free — this is your income |
| **Blacksmith** (the anvil) | **Upgrades** gear: raises core stats and item level | Gold + **Materia** (uncommon tiers) / **Magic Essence** (rare tiers) |
| **Crafter** | **Reforging**, gem crafting, colour transmutation, socket installation | **Mithril** (reforging), **Aether** (gems) |
| **Master Craftsman Braggi** | Gem fusion and **breaking higher-tier gems back down**; converts account-wide resources into small gems **for alts** | Aether |
| **Shrine of Infusion** | **Trait modification** — weapon effect optimisation | — |
| **Storekeeper Keiren** | Material vendor; also **sells gems for Marks of Fellowship** | Marks of Fellowship |

**Aether and Mithril are the scarce materials.** They gate everything that reshapes gear, and they
are the two things you should never spend casually.

## Gems — the acquisition engine

### Where they come from
- **Dungeon drops**, starting in early Contender. Drop rates **increase with difficulty**, maxing at
  around Eternal 20.
- **Reward tracks**, which matter early — you will have gems before you have sockets, since socketed
  gear starts appearing in **Adept**.
- **Storekeeper Keiren**, for Marks of Fellowship. Do not overlook this: it is a reliable, non-random
  source, which is exactly what a low-playtime group wants.

### Tiers and fusion
A strict **3-to-1** ratio, all handled by Braggi:

**3 Small into 1 Large, 3 Large into 1 Splendid, 3 Splendid into 1 Flawless**

Each tier is worth progressively more Gem Power, rising in even steps from Small through Flawless.

**Never discard a weak gem.** Nine Smalls are a Splendid. A gem in the wrong colour is not waste, it
is transmutation stock. Braggi also **breaks high-tier gems back down**, so nothing is a dead end.

**Gem budding** creates a copy of an existing gemstone. Where available, it is the fastest way to
accelerate a specific colour rather than praying for drops.

### Transmutation — changing colour
Three grades of Aether, and choosing the right one is most of the skill:

| Aether | Result | Use it when |
|---|---|---|
| **Unstable** | Random colour, same tier | You are early, flexible, and running a rainbow spread. Cheapest. |
| **Imbued** | Choose from **three** options | The normal working choice mid-game |
| **Arcane** | **Choose any colour** | Save it. Spend only on your final endgame colour, on a Flawless gem. |

The common mistake is burning Arcane Aether early on a Splendid gem for a colour you will re-plan
two weeks later.

### Socketing — the free optimisation
**Always socket your strongest gems into your strongest sockets.** Some items carry bonus gem power
modifiers, and a Flawless gem in a bonus socket is worth strictly more than the same gem elsewhere.
This costs nothing and is routinely missed.

**Gems can be removed and reused freely.** They are permanent investments, not consumables — so
there is no reason to hesitate before socketing.

### Imbued Essence — power without a socket
**Imbued Essence** is a modifier granting **+100 Gem Power of a random colour with no gem required**,
stacking to **+200**. Combined with four sockets, this is how a focused single-colour build reaches
its rank 10 node at all.

### The order that matters most
**Get Seal of the Heskyr before you optimise gem colours.**

Its +25% Gem Power applies to **all sources — socketed gems and Imbued Essence alike**. With it, a
Flawless gem yields **625** power instead of 500 and a +100 Imbued Essence yields **125**. That
changes which track nodes are reachable, and therefore changes the correct colour plan. Transmuting
before you have it means re-planning afterwards, and Arcane Aether spent twice is Arcane Aether
wasted.

It drops from **Scryer's Peak** and **Ruins of Regath**, both easy Adventures. See
[weekly-group.md](weekly-group.md).

## Reforging — buying the item you want

Reforging turns an item you do not want into one you might. Three tiers at the Crafter:

| Tier | What it does | Material | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Standard** | Complete randomisation into same-rarity gear | Cheapest Mithril | A lottery ticket. Fine for excess materials, never a plan. |
| **Imbued** | Reforges, then gives you **three options to choose from** | **Imbued Mithril** | The workhorse. Substantially reduces randomness. |
| **Arcane** | Three choices **within the same equipment slot** | **Arcane Mithril** (epic tier) | Precision. Expensive. Use deliberately. |

### The single most valuable spend in the game

**At Champion+, use Arcane Reforging to get your best-in-slot weapon.** Because Arcane keeps you in
the same slot and gives three choices, it **will** produce your BiS weapon eventually, regardless of
drop luck. This is the recommended first serious material expenditure, and for a group that cannot
farm endlessly it is the most important line in this document: **you can buy determinism.**

Note that all weapon trait nodes require a **300 item level weapon** to fully unlock, which is a late
Eternal target.

### Sub-stats and traits
Crafting materials also **randomise sub-stats** (Power, Spirit, Expertise and so on). Each item holds
a limited number of traits by rarity, and **higher rarity gear rolls stronger versions of the same
effect** — another reason item level and rarity outrank stat perfection until very late.

Weapon trait optimisation happens separately, at the **Shrine of Infusion**.

## Upgrading — the boring one that works

The Blacksmith raises base stats and item level directly.

- **Common items cannot be upgraded.**
- **Costs escalate** with each tier, and **each piece has a maximum upgrade cap**.
- Requires **gold plus Materia** (uncommon) or **Magic Essence** (rare and above).

**Spend gold freely, materials sparingly.** Gold is your renewable resource — scrapping generates it
continuously. Upgrade only pieces you intend to keep. In Eternal, upgrading becomes your **primary**
item level driver, since dropped gear tops out around 270 while your character can reach **330**.

## Tempering with Bloodstones

**Bloodstones** come from the Pinnacle Dungeon, [Xul](../dungeons/xul-the-blood-monolith.md), **once
weekly per difficulty**. They upgrade an item to **Regal rarity at item level 315 while preserving
its existing modifiers**.

Preserving modifiers is the important half. **Temper the item whose modifiers you have already
perfected** — a piece with the right set bonus, the right Imbued Essence and the right socket — not
simply your highest item level piece.

Tempering bonus scales with difficulty: **3x Normal, 5x Hard, 8x Nightmare**.

## The spending ladder

Follow this order. It is built around not wasting scarce materials on gear you will replace.

| Stage | Spend | Do not spend |
|---|---|---|
| **Contender** | **Gold only.** Upgrade anything, replace it happily. | Any Mithril or Aether. None. |
| **Adept** | Gold upgrades. Start **fusing** gems with cheap Aether. Socket everything. | Arcane Aether, Arcane Mithril |
| **Champion** | **Arcane Reforging for your BiS weapon** — the first real spend. Begin Imbued Reforging on stubborn slots. | Arcane Aether on gems you have not final-planned |
| **Paragon** | Chase Legendaries and **Seal of the Heskyr**. Fuse toward Flawless gems. | Colour-locking gems before Heskyr is equipped |
| **Eternal** | Arcane Aether for final colours. Imbued Essence modifiers. Upgrades as the main ilvl driver. Temper with Bloodstones. | Nothing — this is what you saved for |

## What "best configuration" actually looks like

The endgame target for any hero:

1. **BiS Legendary and BiS weapon** (weapon via Arcane Reforging, not luck)
2. **Two sets, four pieces — one of them Seal of the Heskyr**
3. **Correctly statted gear** — at this point, and only at this point, stats outrank raw item level
4. **Four sockets filled with Flawless gems** in your hero's target colours, plus **+200 Imbued Essence**
5. **Your hero's gem track targets reached** — see [gems.md](gems.md) for the per-hero table
6. **Key pieces tempered to Regal 315** with modifiers preserved
7. **A 300+ item level weapon** for full trait node access

For a once-a-week group, expect to reach items 1 through 4 in a season and to treat 5 through 7 as
the stretch. That is a good season.

## Source conflicts, stated plainly

The crafting layer is where public sources disagree most. Where they conflict, this document uses
the most recent Season 3 source and flags it here:

- **Gem power values.** Method gives Small 120 / Large 240 / Splendid 360 / Flawless 480, and states
  **2,640** gem power completes a single-colour track. Icy Veins gives a Flawless gem as **500**
  (625 with Heskyr) and puts the final track node at **1,500**. These are not reconcilable and
  likely reflect different patches. **Treat the ratios as reliable and the absolute numbers as
  approximate**, then confirm in-game.
- **When gems start dropping.** Icy Veins says Contender 4; Method says Contender 7. Either way it is
  early Contender.
- **Material naming.** The Fextralife wiki calls the reforging tiers Shoddy / Serviceable / Intricate
  and the materials **Ingots** (Imbued, Hardened, Arcane, Flawless) with Rough / Imbued / Pure Aether.
  Method uses Standard / Imbued / Arcane reforging with **Mithril** and Unstable / Imbued / Arcane
  Aether. This document uses Method's naming as the more current. **The mechanics match; only the
  names differ.**
