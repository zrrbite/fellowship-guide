# Systems Glossary

Definitions for the terminology used throughout the hero pages.

## Roles

Fellowship uses the classic trinity in **four-person parties**: one Tank, one Healer, two DPS.
Each hero implements its role with a distinct resource system rather than being a reskin — this is
why per-hero stat priorities diverge so sharply.

## Talents

- **14 total points**, earned from dungeon completions and rating progression.
- Points may be spent on **any row** — there is no gating by row depth.
- **Free respec between runs.** Retune single-target vs. AoE per dungeon; this is expected play,
  not min-maxing.
- Most players reach ~**12 points around the time their first Legendary drops**, which is the
  natural handoff point from a generic build to a Legendary-specific build.

Talent tables in this repo are written as `Talent Name (points)` grouped by tier.

## Traits and Modifiers

Higher rarity items roll additional **modifiers**: gem essences, bonus stats, set bonuses, weapon
abilities and relic abilities. Certain items get a guaranteed modifier on top of the random ones.

Trait categories:

| Category | Source | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Major Traits** | Gear (35-point budget) | The big multiplicative effects — Visions of Grandeur, Martial Initiative, Emerald Judgement |
| **Heroic Traits** | Gear (35-point budget) | Secondary stat scaling — Hunter's Focus, Seized Opportunity, Inspired Allegiance |
| **Defensive Traits** | Weapon (5-point budget) | King of the Hill, Iron Spikes, Heart of Stone |
| **Blessings** | Gear | Scale up to **4 ranks**: wearing more of the same one increases the effect up to +4 times |
| **Necklace Attunements** | Necklace | The named traits: The Oak, The Beetle, The Mountain, The Lake, and so on |

**Weapon traits unlock at Champion+.** Unlocking every weapon trait node requires a **300 ilvl
weapon** — an Eternal-stage goal.

### Traits you will see on almost every page

- **Martial Initiative** — on-demand damage reduction; the default Master Trait pick for tanks
- **Visions of Grandeur** — Spirit generation loop; builds your ultimate considerably faster, and
  resets your weapon cooldown at max rank. Core for ultimate-centric DPS.
- **Emerald Judgement** — ~15% Expertise proc; scales with Emerald gem investment (wants 6 ranks
  of green gems to hit meaningful uptime)
- **Amethyst Splinters** — damage plus debuff removal; prevents wasting resource procs
- **King of the Hill** — the single best defensive weapon trait for non-tanks; it prevents one-shots
- **Hunter's Focus** — ~5% Haste when fully stacked; excellent on multi-hit kits
- **Seized Opportunity** — crit buff with high uptime on kits that land many small hits
- **Inspired Allegiance** — group haste, reduces weapon cooldown
- **Brave Machinations** — cooldown reduction on relic/spire effects
- **Iron Spikes** — large armor gain plus damage; tank-only

## Blessings

Named blessings referenced repeatedly:

| Blessing | Effect |
|---|---|
| The Wayfarer | +25% Haste buff roughly every 60 seconds — high uptime on spam-heavy kits |
| The Monarch | Cooldown Acceleration, scales with Haste |
| The Philosopher | ~10% haste / crit / expertise buff; Spirit-build favourite |
| The Trickster | ~10% Crit from Power abilities |
| The Subduer | Cooldown reduction; extra damage at low boss health |
| The Herald | 50 Spirit Points at dungeon start — best in 1-boss dungeons |
| The Celestial | Better than The Herald in 3-boss content |
| The Mystic | +20% damage on main ability at 50% spirit |
| The Usurper | Healing and damage procs from Core abilities |
| The Intrepid | Lower-APM, passive-leaning option |

## Relics

Two relics occupy **2 gear slots**. They provide utility abilities at Rare rarity and above:
healing, party buffs, damage reduction, crowd control, and route tools such as teleportation and
stealth. Collect **one of each type**, then use **Arcane Reforging** on duplicates.

## Necklace Attunements — the common ranking

Broadly consistent across heroes, with role-based swings:

- **The Oak** — largest flat *physical* damage reduction. S-tier for tanks, skip on DPS and healers.
- **The Beetle** — 30% DR against hits dealing 40%+ of max health. The most universally useful
  attunement for non-tanks throughout progression.
- **The Mountain** — 3-second immunity cheat death. Prevents lethal mistakes; near-universal S/A.
- **The Lake** — 20% area damage reduction. S-tier wherever AoE rot damage dominates.
- **The Dragon** — large absorb shield tied to a defensive cast. S-tier on Helena and Meiko.
- **The Stag** — movement speed and a DR window on a defensive cast.
- **The Tower**, **The Dune**, **The River**, **The Boar** — solid mid-tier situational picks.
- **The Viper**, **The Wolf**, **The Shadow** — generally the weakest, situational only.

## Affixes and curses

- **Dungeon curses** begin at **Adept**.
- **True Sight** (new in Season 3) — pick up orbs to reveal marked enemies and gain temporary
  damage and healing bonuses.
- **Stormshield** and **Bloodshards** received significant damage reductions in Season 3.
