# Meiko — Twinsoul (Tank)

**Identity:** A combo-based tank who switches between **Wind** and **Earth** stances, spending
Combo Builders into Finishing Techniques and summoning the Twin Soul for threat management.

## Stat priority

1. **Agility** (main stat)
2. **Expertise = Critical Strike** — genuinely equal; both feed the Peacefield talent synergy
3. **Spirit** — refunds ability charges and grants Spirit Armor buffs
4. **Haste** — weakest stat, but do not zero it out

> "Item level is king in Fellowship as a tank" — main stat and armor dominate everything else.
> Do not pass on a higher ilvl piece for better secondaries until deep Eternal.

## Traits

**Major Traits:** Martial Initiative and Emerald Judgment are the top picks.
Visions of Grandeur and Brave Machinations are the secondary options.

**Heroic Traits:** Inspired Allegiance leads, then Kindling (single-target DPS) and
Seized Opportunity (defence).

**Defensive Traits:** Iron Spikes is the best armor boost against melee damage.
King of the Hill and Treasure Hunter's Delight are also strong.

**Blessings:** The Trickster, The Celestial and The Subduer give the most value.

## Gems

**Priority:** Emerald > Sapphire > Amethyst > Ruby > Diamond > Topaz

**Endgame target:** **10 Emerald, 6 Sapphire, 6 Amethyst**

**Sockets:** Cloak (1), Wrist (1), Rings (2) = 4 gems, plus Seal of the Heskyr's +25%.

## Gear

- **Sets:** Seal of the Heskyr + Sinthara's Veil. Draconic Fury if you need threat.
- **Relics:** one of each type, then reforge duplicates.
