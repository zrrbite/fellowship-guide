# Xavian — Solar Tank

**Identity:** A light/solar tank built around **Shining Halo uptime** and Solar Shield windows.
Unusually for a tank, Xavian is a genuine crit-scaling character — his gem plan is Amethyst-first,
which no other tank runs.

## Stat priority

1. **Strength** — primary attribute, greatest impact
2. **Crit** — target **~20%**, after the Haste and Expertise thresholds are met
3. **Haste** — **22% minimum**; core stat reducing GCD and ability cooldowns. You may exceed it.
4. **Expertise** — amplifies damage, healing and absorption
5. **Spirit** — **avoid.** Already covered by his talents; itemising for it is wasted budget.

> Order of operations: reach **22% Haste** and your Expertise floor first, *then* push Crit to ~20%.
> Do not stack Crit before the Haste threshold.

## Talent build — Blades Build (primary)

| Tier | Talents |
|---|---|
| 1 | Sun's Touch (2), Grand Halo (2), Solar Burn (2) |
| 2 | Tempered Descent (1), Light's Oath (1), Rising Sun (1) |
| 3 | Imposing Presence (2), Celestial Favor (2), Gleaming Strikes (2) |
| 4 | Sun's Verdict (1), Magic Ward (1), Hallowed Shield (1) |
| 5 | Vanguard of Vengeance (3), Invictus (3), Golden Hour (3) |
| 6 | Zenith Technique (1), Healing Halo (1), Shifting Spectrum (1) |

**Early game:** prioritise talents that extend **Shining Halo uptime** via Brilliant Flash casts.

**Mid game:** Golden Hour becomes important before you transition to the endgame setup.

**On acquiring your BiS Legendary weapon:** drop **Imposing Presence** and redistribute into
Celestial Favor, or combine Shifting Spectrum + Magic Ward.

### Alternative: Tanky Shield build

Replace Imposing Presence with Celestial Favor, and run Light's Oath + Magic Ward. This is the
build for **Sandworn Bands of the Fortress**. Choose Blades for damage, Shield for survivability —
Xavian genuinely supports both and the choice is playstyle-driven.

|---|---|
| The Trickster | 10% Crit from Power abilities |
| The Usurper | Healing and damage procs from Core abilities |

| The Herald | 50 Spirit Points at dungeon start |

**Necklace Attunements**
- **S:** The Oak (largest flat physical DR), The Stag (Solar Shield grants 30% movement speed and
  12% damage reduction for 8 seconds)
- **A:** The Mountain, The River, The Dune — only a marginal step below S

## Gems

**Priority:** Amethyst > Sapphire > Emerald > Diamond > Ruby > Topaz

**Endgame target:** **10 Amethyst, 6 Sapphire, 6 Emerald**

**Sockets:** 4 total — 1 cloak, 1 wrist, 2 rings.

Xavian is the only tank whose primary gem track is Amethyst. This follows directly from his Crit
scaling; do not copy Helena's or Meiko's Emerald-first plan onto him.

## Gear

- **Sets:** Seal of the Heskyr + Sinthara's Veil. Draconic Fury is a real consideration here given
  his crit scaling — the +4% Crit is not wasted on Xavian the way it would be on Helena.
- **Legendaries:** Sandworn Bands of the Fortress pushes you toward the Shield build.
