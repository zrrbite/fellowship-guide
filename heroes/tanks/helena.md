# Helena — Warmaster (Tank)

**Identity:** Toughness plus tight cooldown cycling. Helena converts offence into survivability
through frequent ability resets and shield absorb technology. She is the most cooldown-dependent
tank in the roster, which is why her entire build is bent toward cooldown reduction.

## Stat priority

1. **Item Level / Strength** — primary scaling for both damage and survivability
2. **Haste** — reduces GCD and the Shields Up cooldown; synergises with the Veteran of War passive
3. **Spirit** — decreases Ultimate cooldown; enables double CDR from Veteran of War
4. **Expertise** — increases damage output *and* absorb size
5. **Critical Strike** — mostly damage, but absorbs can crit for double effectiveness

> The Haste/Spirit pairing is not interchangeable filler. Veteran of War double-dips on them, which
> is what makes Helena's Emerald + Sapphire gem plan work.

## Traits

**Major (Master) Traits**
- **S:** Martial Initiative — the best Master Trait, especially alongside the Emerald gem capstone
- **S:** Visions of Grandeur — only with heavy Spirit/Sapphire investment
- **A:** Emerald Judgement — potent AoE crowd control and damage

**Heroic Traits**
- **Best:** Inspired Allegiance — especially with Martial Initiative or the Darkfrost Arms weapon
- **Secondary:** Kindling or Willful Momentum for single-target damage once Haste hits diminishing returns

**Defensive Traits**
- **S:** Iron Spikes — massive armor, strong damage, and it feeds Gleaming Shield
- **A:** Treasure Hunter's Delight (high-rank value), King of the Hill (maximum DR option)

**Blessings**
- **Core:** The Subduer (CDR for Grand Melee), The Philosopher (Spirit-heavy builds),
  The Wayfarer (high haste uptime)
- **Synergy:** The Monarch pairs with The Subduer for enormous Grand Melee cooldown reduction

**Necklace Attunements**
- **S:** The Oak (largest flat physical DR), The Dragon (huge absorb on Shields Up)
- **A:** The Mountain, The Stag, The Viper

## Gems

**Early priority:** Emerald > Sapphire > Diamond > Amethyst > Topaz > Ruby

**Endgame target:** **10 Emerald + 10 Sapphire.** This requires roughly **400 additional gem power
from Imbued Essence modifiers** on top of four Flawless gems with Seal of the Heskyr.

**Viable alternative (before you can reach double rank 10):** 6 Sapphire, 6 Emerald, 6 Amethyst,
6 Ruby or Diamond.

## Gear

- **Sets:** Seal of the Heskyr + Sinthara's Veil (magic DR). Draconic Fury if threat is a problem;
  Torment of Bael'Aurum for a cheat death during progression.
- **Relics:** two slots — take one of each type first, then reforge duplicates.
