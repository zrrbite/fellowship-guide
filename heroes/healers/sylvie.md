# Sylvie — Daughter of the Forest (Healer)

**Identity:** Proactive, pet-driven healing. Flutterflies, Life Petals and Boomshrooms do the work;
Sylvie casts **minimal direct heals** and instead pre-seeds healing-over-time before damage lands.
Playing her reactively is the single most common mistake.

## Stat priority

1. **Item Level / Intellect**
2. **Haste** — reduces GCD, increases Flutterfly healing tick rate, and reduces Heart Bloom recharge
   time with the Flower Power talent
3. **Spirit** — decreases ultimate cooldown, refunds mana, spawns Prickly Vines, and buffs Life
   Petal healing via Symbiosis
4. **Expertise** — flat increase to healing and damage
5. **Crit** — the random nature of it makes it less desirable, but it is still good

> Stat ratings have **diminishing returns**. Prioritise by content difficulty rather than following
> the list rigidly — in harder content, survivability stats climb the order.

## Traits

### Cloak build — Mad Gardener's Shawl
**Blessings:** 4/4 The Philosopher, The Monarch, 4/4 Emerald Judgement, Martial Initiative,
Visions of Grandeur (1 point)

**Major Traits:** Emerald Judgement, Martial Initiative, Visions of Grandeur

### Ring build — Loop of Unyielding Bloom
Similar allocation, but **The Intrepid (1+ points)** replaces heavy Philosopher investment. This is
the lower-APM, more passive playstyle. Choose it if you find Sylvie's pet management overwhelming.

**Heroic Traits (pool):** Hidden Power, Hunter's Focus, Inspired Allegiance, Kindling,
Navigator's Intuition, Patient Soul, Seized Opportunity, Vengeful Soul, Willful Momentum

**Defensive Traits (pool):** Divine Mediation, First Man Standing, Grounded Spirit, Heart of Stone,
Iron Spikes, King of the Hill, Latent Resurgence, Stalwart Readiness, Treasure Hunter's Delight

## Gems

**Early game — spread Red / Green / Blue:**

| Colour | Key nodes |
|---|---|
| **Ruby** | Blessing of the Conqueror, Titan's Blood, Unyielding Vitality, Champion's Heart, Might of the Minotaur |
| **Emerald** | Blessing of the Commander, Tactician's Acumen, Sentinel's Bastion, Vanguard's Resolve, First Strike |
| **Sapphire** | Blessing of the Prophet, Oracle's Foresight, Resonating Soul, Mystic's Intuition, Ancestral Surge |

**Gem power priority:** Red > Green > Blue, then Purple and Yellow.

**Endgame target:** **10 Green / 10 Blue**, with remaining gem power into Purple / Yellow / Red.

- **Green** provides CDR, absorb shields, and Rank 2 First Strike via Emerald Judgement
- **Blue** — Spirit is a very strong stat for Sylvie, and **Resonating Soul** is her best defensive node
- **Yellow** — **Rogue's Resurgence** is a very strong defensive node
- **Avoid White**, except possibly White 3 to fill an otherwise unused slot

## Gear

- **Sets:** Seal of the Heskyr + Haunting Lament. Torment of Bael'Aurum for progression safety.
- **Legendaries:** Mad Gardener's Shawl (cloak, higher APM) or Loop of Unyielding Bloom (ring,
  passive). Your Legendary decides your build, not the reverse.
