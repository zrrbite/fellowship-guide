# Aeona — Time-Manipulating Healer

**Identity:** A Chrona builder/spender healer built around **Unfolding Doom uptime**. She is the
most cooldown-reduction-hungry healer, and the only one whose gem plan runs Ruby first.

## Stat priority

1. **Item level / Intellect**
2. **Haste** — **breakpoint at 32.28%+**, where abilities gain an extra damage tick
3. **Crit** — doubles damage and healing, and grants **50% more Chrona on builder crits**
4. **Expertise**
5. **Spirit**

> The **32.28% Haste breakpoint** is the single most important number on this page. Reaching it is
> worth more than a full item level tier of raw stats. Chase it deliberately.

**Flex (3 points):** Edge of Ruin (2), Chrona Tap (1)

**Additional talents in the wider build:** Erasure (2), Synchronicity (2), Paradoxical Twist (3),
Oblivion's Embrace (3), Karmic Dispersion (1), Echoes of Divinity (1), Vestige of Eternity (1)

**Baseline essentials before spending flex points:** Uchronia, Entropic Burst, Hastening Doom,
Continuum Shift, Resonant Fate, Echoes of Divinity.

### Variants

| Situation | Change |
|---|---|
| **Heavy group-wide damage** | Keep Edge of Ruin + Karmic Dispersion oriented toward AoE healing rather than single-target |
| **Single-target focus** | Swap the AoE emphasis back into Edge of Ruin |
| **Need survivability** | Replace Edge of Ruin and Karmic Dispersion with **Oblivion's Embrace** for extra shielding |

## Traits

**Master Traits:** Martial Initiative (on-demand DR), Emerald Judgement (15% Expertise proc),
Visions of Grandeur (Spirit generation loop)

**Heroic Traits:** Hunter's Focus (~5% Haste when stacked), Seized Opportunity (Crit synergy)

**Defensive Traits:** King of the Hill (consistent value), Heart of Stone (secondary)

**Necklace Attunements**
- **S:** The Stag, The Lake
- **A:** The Boar, The Dragon, The Mountain
- **Situational:** The Wolf, The Shadow, The Tower, The Oak, The Viper

## Gems

**Priority:** Ruby → Amethyst → Topaz → Emerald → Sapphire → Diamond

**Endgame setup (with Seal of the Heskyr):**

| Colour | Node | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| Ruby | 6 | 6% Intellect via Might of the Minotaur II |
| Amethyst | 6 | 15% Crit via Sealed Fate II |
| Topaz | 6 | Haste — feeds the 32.28% breakpoint |
| Emerald | 10 | **12% cooldown reduction** for Unfolding Doom uptime |

Note this is a *four-track* endgame plan, not a single-colour collapse. Aeona wants breadth because
each track feeds a different part of her loop.

## Gear

- **Sets:** Seal of the Heskyr + Haunting Lament (+4% Spirit, +15% max mana).
  Torment of Bael'Aurum if you are dying during progression pushes.
- **Relics:** one of each type.
