# Vigour — Lightshaper (Healer)

**Identity:** A Radiant Runes builder/spender healer combining direct healing with a rune system and
the **Avatar of Light** spirit form. The most reactive healer in the roster, and the one with the
clearest single-colour gem plan.

## Stat priority

1. **Item level / Intellect**
2. **Haste** — lowers cooldowns and GCDs, and shortens casts
3. **Spirit** — decreases ultimate cooldown, provides mana refunds
4. **Expertise = Critical Strike** — genuinely equal. Crit doubles damage and healing *and*
   interacts with Rune generation.

## Traits

**Major Traits, in priority order:**
1. **Martial Initiative** — BiS in nearly all situations
2. **Visions of Grandeur** — 1 point captures the full Spirit generation benefit
3. **Emerald Judgement** — 2+ points if you are running the Emerald 6 gem setup
4. **Amethyst Splinters** — 1 point for damage plus debuff removal

**Heroic Traits:** Inspired Allegiance (group Haste, reduces weapon cooldown),
Patient Soul (5% max HP for a single trait slot, excellent value),
Kindling and Hunter's Focus for single-target damage

**Defensive Traits:** King of the Hill (primary), Latent Resurgence and First Man Standing
(1-point budget options)

**Blessings**
- **The Monarch 4/4** — primary choice; keeps Runic Proliferation ready for almost any healing mechanic
- **The Wayfarer** — high uptime through core ability spam with Haste investment
- **The Herald** — best for 1-boss dungeons; **The Celestial** is better for 3-boss content

**Necklace Attunements**
- **S:** The Mountain, The Lake — both prevent lethal mistakes
- **A:** The Boar, The Dragon, The Stag — on-demand defensive power

## Gems

**Primary build (healing-focused):**

| Colour | Node | What it buys |
|---|---|---|
| **Sapphire** | **10** | Main goal — duration and main stat during Avatar of Light |
| Topaz | 6 | Haste scaling with core abilities |
| Emerald | 6 (+2 Emerald Judgement) | ~60% uptime on the First Strike buff |
| Ruby | 6 | Survivability and boss damage amplification |

**Alternative (damage-focused):** Diamond 9 + Amethyst 6 to maximise baseline damage. **Not
recommended outside Eternal 60+ difficulty.**

> Useful farming flexibility: **5 gem imbues of a specific colour are interchangeable with the gems
> themselves.** If a colour will not drop, buy it with imbues instead.

## Gear

- **Sets:** Seal of the Heskyr + Haunting Lament (+4% Spirit, +15% max mana, which directly extends
  Vigour's sustain window).
- **Legendaries:** Cloak (general progression), Ring of Harmonizing Refractions (defensive
  Empyrean Sands build).
