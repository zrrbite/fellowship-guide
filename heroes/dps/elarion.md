# Elarion — Sentinel of Starlight (DPS)

**Identity:** A fast-firing archer who can use **all of his abilities while moving**. Focus
generation, Lunarlight Marks, and a kit flexible enough to switch between Highwind Arrow and
Heartseeker Barrage playstyles.

**Interrupt: Disrupt** — **30 yard range, 20s cooldown**, instant. The longest-ranged interrupt confirmed in the roster, which makes Elarion the safest choice for a banked boss kick. All interrupts lock the target out of casting for **4 seconds**, except against dedicated boss timers. Plan on a **~20s cooldown**; see [interrupts.md](../../docs/interrupts.md).

## Stat priority

1. **Item level / Agility**
2. **Haste / Spirit** — Haste lowers cast times and increases focus generation; Spirit effectively
   decreases ultimate cooldown
3. **Crit / Expertise** — Crit gives a chance to double ability damage; Expertise is a flat increase

Elarion is unusual in having a genuinely paired second tier rather than a strict ranking. Chase
Haste and Spirit together.

> **Barrage build thresholds:** the Barrage variant becomes optimal at **Haste >20%** and
> **Spirit 30-35%**, with 10b gear and the Signet equipped.

### Variant — Spirit Barrage

Identical talent allocation. Swap the legendary to **Signet of Starstriker's Ascent**, which resets
Heartseeker Barrage. Only make this switch once you clear the Haste and Spirit thresholds above.

## Traits

**Major Traits**
- **Visions of Grandeur** — builds your ultimate considerably faster, and resets your weapon
  cooldown at max rank
- **Emerald Judgement (1 pt)** — synergises with First Strike for increased uptime
- **Amethyst Splinters (1 pt)** — prevents wasting Lunarlight Marks

**Heroic Traits:** Willful Momentum (Spirit buff on spirit refunds); Kindling, Patient Soul or
Seized Opportunity as situational choices

**Defensive Traits:** **King of the Hill** — simply the best defensive that can roll on the weapon
tree. Divine Mediation, Grounded Spirit or Heart of Stone as secondary.

**Blessings:** The Philosopher (10% haste/crit/expertise), The Monarch (Cooldown Acceleration
scaling with haste), The Mystic (20% damage on main ability at 50% spirit), The Trickster (10% crit
on power abilities), The Herald (50 starting spirit)

**Necklace Attunements — S-tier**
- The Mountain — 3-second immunity cheat death
- The Beetle — 30% DR against hits dealing 40%+ of max health
- The Dragon — 40% shield lasting 40 seconds

## Single-target damage

**Priority order:**

1. Skystrider's Grace
2. **Event Horizon** (ultimate)
3. Skystrider's Supremacy
4. Lunarlight Mark
5. **Starfall Volley**
6. **Heartseeker Barrage** — Barrage build only
7. Celestial Shot — **procs only**
8. Empowered Multishot
9. Highwind Arrow
10. Focused Shot

**Starfall Volley and Heartseeker Barrage are where the damage actually is.** Everything above them
in the list exists to let you cast them more often.

**Elarion does not pool.** The guidance is to *get everything on cooldown* — holding abilities for a
better moment is a loss, because Event Horizon grants **30% haste plus cooldown reduction that scales
with haste**, and reduces Focus costs. The more you have spinning when it lands, the more Starfall
Volleys you fit inside it.

**At Champion, stay on Highwind Arrow.** The Barrage variant is gated behind **Haste >20% and Spirit
30-35% with 10b gear and the Signet** — Eternal-tier numbers. Swapping early is a straight downgrade,
not an early adoption.

### Why Elarion is the best DPS in the roster for movement fights

He can use **every ability while moving**. On a fight that forces repositioning every few seconds,
this is not a convenience — it is a damage stat. Every other DPS pays a uptime tax that Elarion does
not, so his *relative* value rises the more a fight moves you around. See
[Wyrmheart](../../dungeons/wyrmheart.md#squeezing-out-the-damage), where he should also be the player
banking the Frigid Mists interrupt: **Disrupt has 30 yards of range**, so he can hold the kick without
ever standing where the knock-ups happen.

**Threat:** on long single-target fights a well-geared Elarion can out-threat the tank. **Draconic
Deceit** (+4% Crit, **−15% threat**) is the fix, and it drops in Wyrmheart and Godfall Quarry.

## AoE damage

**Switches on at 3+ targets.** The list is the single-target one with two insertions:

1. Skystrider's Grace
2. Event Horizon
3. Skystrider's Supremacy
4. Lunarlight Mark
5. Starfall Volley
6. Heartseeker Barrage
7. Celestial Shot — procs only
8. **Empowered Multishot** — moves up significantly
9. **Multishot** — enters the priority at all
10. Highwind Arrow
11. Focused Shot

**The one thing to actually remember: pair Lunarlight Mark with Barrage whenever possible.** That
pairing is AoE-specific and is where the multi-target damage comes from — the rest is the same shape
as your boss rotation with Multishot slotted in.
