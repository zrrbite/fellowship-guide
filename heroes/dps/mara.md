# Mara — Hungering Blade (DPS)

**Identity:** An assassin-style melee DPS. Combo Points spent on finishers such as Queen's Fang,
layered with poison and hemorrhage effects.

## Stat priority — by build

| Build | Priority | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Hemotoxin** | Crit > Expertise >> Spirit > Haste | Haste synergies are the primary focus in the current build |
| **Starter Malevolence** | Crit > Expertise >> Spirit > Haste | Finisher-focused damage |
| **Spirit Malevolence (endgame)** | Spirit > Crit >> Expertise > Haste | Target **45-50% Spirit as a hard cap**, hold **5-7% Haste**, maximise remaining Crit |

**Weapon stat rolls:**
- Hemotoxin — 2 Haste rolls
- Malevolence — 2 main stat rolls
- Spirit Malevolence — Crit or Spirit, to balance the build

**Playstyle:** DoT-focused damage through Hemorrhaging Strike.

### Variant — Spirit Malevolence (endgame)

The same talent allocation, optimised for high Spirit scaling. Points in **Caustic Wounds** and
**Seething Burst** can be swapped into **Corrosive Spill** or **Feed the Queen** depending on
whether you need single-target or area/funnel damage.

## Traits

**Major Traits:** Emerald Judgement, Sapphire Aurastone, Ruby Storm, Heroic Brand

**Heroic Traits:** Seized Opportunity (weapon), Patient Soul (defensive alternative),
Hunter's Focus, Inspired Allegiance

**Defensive Traits:** Emerald Judgement as a defensive swap; The Subduer and The Monarch for
survivability in late-game content

**Blessings:** customise to the build — primarily offensive choices for damage-focused setups, with
defensive options available when the content demands it.

**Necklace Attunements**

| Tier | Attunements |
|---|---|
| S | The Beetle, The Lake |
| A | The Mountain, The Tower, The Dune |
| B | The Shadow, The Dragon, The Stag, The Wolf |

> The Beetle is by far the most universally useful attunement throughout progression: it gives
> damage reduction on exactly the hits where it matters most.

## Gear

- **Sets:** Seal of the Heskyr + Death's Grasp. Drakheim's Absolution (+20% primary stat on Spirit
  Ability) is a particularly good fit for the Spirit Malevolence build.
- **Relics:** one of each type, then reforge duplicates.
