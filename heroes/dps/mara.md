# Mara — Hungering Blade (DPS)

**Identity:** An assassin-style melee DPS. Combo Points spent on finishers such as Queen's Fang,
layered with poison and hemorrhage effects.

**Interrupt: Kick.** All interrupts lock the target out of casting for **4 seconds**, except against dedicated boss timers. Plan on a **~20s cooldown**; see [interrupts.md](../../docs/interrupts.md).

## Single-target damage

**Queen's Fang is your single-target finisher** — use it when fighting **2 or fewer targets**, or **5
or fewer with Feed The Queen** talented. **Backstab** is your combo point builder on a boss; Skittering
Blades is an 8+ target tool and has no place in a boss rotation.

**Maintain the debuff floor:**

- **Hemorrhaging Strike** on the target — at 3 targets or fewer it runs its **full duration**, so
  there is no reason to let it lapse
- **Seething Poison**, applied with **Widow's Bite** from Brooding Shadows, if it is not already up

**Do not overcap.** Combo points, Energy **and charges** all count — Mara leaks damage through
overcapping more than through rotational mistakes. Every wasted builder is a finisher you did not cast.

**Single target versus AoE:** the rotation genuinely changes shape. On a boss you build with Backstab
and spend on Queen's Fang; in AoE, Arachnid Assault becomes the preferred spend. Do not carry the AoE
habit into a single-target fight.

## AoE damage

Mara has the most target-count breakpoints in the roster. Learn the two that matter:

| Targets | Builder | Finisher |
|---|---|---|
| **1-2** | Backstab | **Queen's Fang** |
| **3+** | Skittering Blades *(2+ with Feed The Queen)* | **Arachnid Assault** *(6+ with Feed The Queen)* |
| **8+** | **Skittering Blades** becomes the primary builder outright | Arachnid Assault |

**Widow's Bite** stays useful at **4 or fewer** targets and becomes secondary in bigger pulls.
**Hemorrhaging Strike** runs its full duration at **3 targets or fewer** — apply it to everything
regardless of count if you are running a Hemorrhaging-Strike-focused setup.

**Both finishers feed Malevolence**, and alternating between them builds stacking damage buffs — so
the swap between Queen's Fang and Arachnid Assault is not just a damage-per-target calculation.

> **Source conflict:** the Skittering Blades threshold is given as both *3 or more* and *8 or more*
> targets in different lines of the same guide, likely reflecting different talent setups. Treat 3+ as
> the point it enters the rotation and 8+ as the point it dominates.
