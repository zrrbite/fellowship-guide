# Rime — Harbinger of Winter (DPS)

**Identity:** A ranged caster running an Anima → Winter Orbs system, pivoting between priority
damage and area damage. Rime is the clearest example of a hero who genuinely wants the **rainbow
gem shape** at endgame rather than a single-colour collapse.

## Stat priority

1. **Item level / Intellect**
2. **Haste = Crit = Expertise** — all three are of equal value
3. **Spirit** — lowest priority, due to diminishing returns from Winter's Blessing and a weak
   Spirit Ultimate

**Details:** Haste reduces cooldowns (especially Cold Snap) and increases anima generation. Crit
doubles ability damage but does **not** affect Winter's Blessing healing. Expertise is a
straightforward damage and healing increase.

> Because the three secondaries are equal, Rime is the least itemisation-sensitive hero in the
> roster. Take the higher item level piece essentially always.

## Traits

**Major Traits (S-tier)**
- **Emerald Judgement** — requires 6 ranks of green gems for 15% Expertise uptime
- **Brave Machinations (1 pt)** — 15% CDR on Asha's Chronoshift Spire

**Heroic Traits (S-tier)**
- **Hunter's Focus 4/4** — Rime maintains 5 stacks easily, for a large haste gain
- **Seized Opportunity** — high uptime on the crit buff from her many small hits

**Defensive Traits (S-tier):** **King of the Hill** — prevents one-shots

**Blessings (S-tier):** The Monarch (6% cooldown acceleration at late game),
The Subduer (extra damage at low boss health)

**Necklace Attunements (S-tier):** The Mountain — prevents lethal damage

## Gems

**Priority:** Sapphire > Emerald > Amethyst = Ruby = Topaz > Diamond

**Endgame configuration — 4× rank 6 rainbow (600 gem power per track):**

| Colour | Node | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Sapphire | 6 | Ancestral Surge II |
| Emerald | 6 | First Strike II — synergises with the Emerald Judgement trait |
| Amethyst | 6 | Sealed Fate II |
| Ruby **or** Topaz | 6 | Depending on build focus |

**Sockets:** 4 total (Cloak, Wrists, both Rings), with Seal of the Heskyr's +25%.

Note the deliberate design: Rime does **not** want a rank 10 track. Four rank-6 nodes give her more
than one deep track, because Emerald 6 is what unlocks the Emerald Judgement synergy and there is
no comparable payoff at rank 10 for her kit.

## Gear

- **Sets:** Seal of the Heskyr + Death's Grasp. Draconic Might is a reasonable alternative for
  burst ceiling.
- **Legendaries:** Skandi's Bands of Endless Winter, Eldrin Signet of Undulating Spirits,
  Drakesblood Tapestry — each defines a build.
