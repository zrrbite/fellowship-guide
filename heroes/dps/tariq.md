# Tariq — Stormbreaker (DPS)

**Identity:** A melee DPS with a **visible Swing Timer**. Tariq builds and spends Fury, times Heavy
Strikes against the swing timer, and uses Thunder Call to open burst windows. He is the most
timing-dependent hero in the roster — gear improves him, but execution defines him.

## Stat priority

1. **Strength** — main stat has the greatest impact on damage
2. **Haste** — reduces GCD and cooldowns for rotational abilities, and **increases PPM proc rates**
3. **Expertise** — a direct damage increase
4. **Crit** — a chance to double ability damage
5. **Spirit** — enables spirit procs for fury refunds and Rising Spirit stacking

> Haste is worth more on Tariq than its raw damage suggests, because it raises the proc rate of
> every PPM effect he carries, including set bonuses.

## Talent build — Lightning (recommended for beginners)

This build takes mostly **passive** damage talents, so you can concentrate on mastering the real
skill: building and spending Fury at the right swing-timer moments.

| Tier | Talents |
|---|---|
| 2 | Left Hand Path (2), Ride the Lightning (2), Square Hammer (2) |
| 3 | High Road (1), Blood & Thunder (1), Bloodline (1) |
| 4 | The Motherload (2), Mouth for War (2), Thunderstruck (2) |
| 5 | Pneuma (1), Sledgehammer (1), Far Beyond Driven (1) |
| 6 | Kill Em All (3), Ace of Spades (3), Schism (3), Them Bones (1), Crack the Sky (1), Killing in the Name (1) |

**Levelling priority:**
1. Foundational damage — Left Hand Path, Ride the Lightning, Square Hammer
2. Chain Lightning amplifiers — The Motherload, Thunderstruck, Crack the Sky
3. Fury generation and utility

**Flex:** if you prefer to avoid Leap Smash, swap **High Road** for **Bloodline** with no
significant fury generation loss.

### Variant — Schism (advanced)

Focuses on Schism procs (a flat 25% chance) for priority damage in trash. Includes **Sledgehammer**
for cleave scaling and **Mouth for War** for empowered spender usage.

## Traits

**Major Traits (S-tier)**
- **Visions of Grandeur (1-4 ranks)** — core, for the ultimate reset
- **Martial Initiative** — strong with ultimate-focused builds

**Heroic Traits (A-tier)**
- Navigator's Intuition and Hunter's Focus — secondary stat scaling beneficiaries
- Hidden Power and Vengeful Soul — multiplicative primary stat increases

**Defensive Traits:** King of the Hill and First Man Standing are the highest priority

**Blessings (A-tier)**
- **The Mystic** — spender damage, with Schism builds
- **The Herald** — works synergistically with the Pneuma talent

**Necklace Attunements**
- **S:** The Beetle, The Mountain — the mishap-saviours
- **A:** The Wolf, The Shadow, The Lake — breadth of survivability

## Gems

**Priority, in order:**
1. **Sapphire 6** — Ancestral Surge II
2. **Amethyst 6** — Sealed Fate II
3. **Ruby 6** — Might of the Minotaur II
4. **Topaz 6 or Emerald 6** — depending on build

**Endgame:** four tier 4 gems total. Either reach **rank 6 across multiple colours** (the default),
or **rank 10 in one colour** if you have Seal of the Heskyr's +25% and the Imbued Essence modifiers
to fund it.

**Sockets:** Cloak, Wrists, both Rings — four total.

## Gear

- **Sets:** Seal of the Heskyr + Death's Grasp. **Dark Prophecy** is unusually strong on Tariq,
  because its 25% haste directly accelerates his swing-timer rotation and raises his own PPM procs.
- **Relics:** one of each type, then reforge duplicates.
